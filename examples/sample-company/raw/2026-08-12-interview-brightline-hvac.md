# Interview: Brightline HVAC

- Date: 2026-08-12
- Interviewer: Priya Nair (Senior PM, Mobile)
- Who: owner of Brightline HVAC, 8 technicians, residential and light
  commercial HVAC
- Account: Team plan, customer since July 2026
- Length: 40 minutes, video call

## Notes

They moved from paper work orders to Crewline in July. The office manager
books jobs; the owner still goes out on calls two days a week.

Biggest problem by far is signal. A lot of their work is in basements,
crawl spaces and older buildings. The app shows a spinner and the tech can't
see the job notes or close the job.

> "My guys lose signal in basements and crawl spaces every day. They write
> it on the back of the work order and re-type it at night."

The office manager spends about five hours a week re-typing job notes and
parts used from paper. He said they pay her overtime for it.

They tried telling techs to close jobs from the truck after they come up,
but techs forget, and then the invoice goes out late.

Second problem: the accounting sync sometimes creates duplicate customers,
and they fix them by hand once a week.

Asked what would make them leave: "If the app doesn't work in a basement by
the fall, I'll look at something else. I'm already getting emails from
JobHarbor."
