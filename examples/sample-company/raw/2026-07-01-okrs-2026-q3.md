# Q3 2026 goals

Owner: Dan Reyes, VP Product
Written: 2026-07-01
Period: 2026-07-01 to 2026-09-30

Company goals come from Maya. Team goals were set by each product lead with
me. Committed means we expect to hit it. Aspirational means we'd be happy
with 70%.

## Company

**Objective: Grow net revenue retention.** Set by Maya Okafor.

- KR1 (committed): Net revenue retention from 96% to 101%.
- KR2 (committed): Cut 90-day churn of new teams from 38% to 30%.

## Mobile team (lead: Priya Nair)

**Objective: Technicians rely on Crewline in the field.**

- KR1 (committed): Launch offline mode by September 30.
- KR2 (aspirational): Weekly active technicians as a share of paid seats,
  from 41% to 55%.

**Objective: Make the Android tablet app great.**

- KR1 (aspirational): Improve our app store rating.

## Billing team (lead: Tom Becker)

**Objective: Owners get paid faster.**

- KR1 (committed): Median days from invoice sent to invoice paid, from 19
  to 14.

## Definitions

- Active team: a team that scheduled at least one job in the week.
- Weekly active technician: a technician who completed at least one job in
  the mobile app in the week.
- Net revenue retention: this quarter's recurring revenue from teams that
  were paying a year ago, divided by their recurring revenue a year ago.
