# JobHarbor release notes, October 2026 (copied)

Copied from JobHarbor's public changelog by Priya Nair on 2026-10-20.
Priya's note: "They shipped offline before we hit GA with everyone. And
routing on the mid plan is new."

---

## October 7, 2026

- **Offline mode, all plans.** Technicians can open jobs, add notes and
  photos, and close jobs with no signal. Everything syncs when they're back
  online.
- Faster job search on mobile.

## October 15, 2026

- **Route planning now included on the Crew plan.** Previously Business
  only. Crew is $119/month for up to 12 technicians.
- New: switch from another field service app in one click. Import your
  customers, jobs and price book.

## October 18, 2026

- Bug fixes for photo uploads on older Android phones.
