# Crewline strategy, H2 2026 to H1 2027

From: Maya Okafor, CEO, Tidewater Software
To: all staff
Date: 2026-06-02

Team, this is the strategy we agreed at the leadership offsite on May 28.
Dan Reyes (VP Product) and I own it. We will review it every quarter, next
at the start of September.

## Where we are

Crewline schedules jobs, dispatches technicians and sends invoices for small
field service companies. We have about 1,100 paying teams. Most are HVAC,
plumbing and electrical contractors in the US.

Growth is fine at the top of the funnel. The problem is what happens after
signup: 38% of new teams cancel within their first 90 days. When we ask why,
the two answers we hear most are that setup takes too long and that the
technicians never really start using the mobile app, so the office keeps
working from paper and phone calls.

## The challenge

New teams don't get their technicians onto the mobile app, so they never see
the value and leave in the first 90 days.

## Where we play

US HVAC, plumbing and electrical contractors with 2 to 25 technicians.

## How we win

The fastest path from a booked job to a paid invoice, with a mobile app that
technicians actually use in the field.

## Focus areas

1. Technician adoption of the mobile app.
2. Getting paid: from invoice sent to money in the bank.

## Bets for the next two quarters

- Offline mode for the mobile app, so technicians can work in basements,
  crawl spaces and rural areas without signal.
- Automatic invoice reminders, so owners stop chasing payments by phone.

## What we won't do

- Companies with more than 50 technicians. They need SSO, multi-location
  dispatch and custom contracts, and they pull the roadmap away from small
  teams.
- Construction project management.
- Markets outside the US in 2026.

## Pricing

No price changes this half. Route optimization stays a Pro-only feature. It
is the main reason teams upgrade from Team to Pro, and Dan and I decided to
keep it there.

## How we'll know

Net revenue retention and 90-day churn of new teams. Dan will set the
quarterly goals with the product leads.

Maya
