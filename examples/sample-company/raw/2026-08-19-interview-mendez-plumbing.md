# Interview: Mendez Plumbing

- Date: 2026-08-19
- Interviewer: Tom Becker (PM, Billing)
- Who: owner of Mendez Plumbing, 3 technicians, residential plumbing
- Account: Starter plan, customer since March 2025
- Length: 30 minutes, phone

## Notes

Happy with scheduling. Uses the mobile app himself every day.

I asked about connectivity because the mobile team wanted to know. He said:

> "Offline? Never had a problem. We're mostly in houses, above ground. If
> the signal's bad I just walk outside."

The real pain is getting paid. About a third of his invoices sit unpaid for
more than 30 days. He built his own spreadsheet to track who owes what, and
every Friday his wife calls the late ones.

> "If Crewline sent the reminders for me, I'd pay more for it. Honestly I'd
> pay for that before anything else."

He doesn't want to text customers from his personal phone for payment
reminders. He wants it to come from the business.

Didn't know the Team plan has accounting sync. Probably not worth it for
him at 3 techs.
