# Meeting notes: route optimization packaging

- Date: 2026-10-28
- Attendees: Maya Okafor (CEO), Dan Reyes (VP Product), Sam Ortiz (Sales
  lead), Tom Becker (PM, Billing), Raj Patel (Analyst)
- Notes by: Tom Becker

## Why we met

JobHarbor added route planning to its Crew plan on October 15. Sam says we
lost Team-plan customers over it.

## What we heard

- Sam: 14 Team-plan teams cancelled in September and October and named
  routing or JobHarbor in the cancellation survey. Sam pulled this from the
  cancellation survey; the export isn't attached.
- Raj: 9% of Team-plan teams upgraded to Pro in the last 12 months. In the
  upgrade survey, most named route optimization as the reason. He'll send
  the numbers.
- Dan: Arc Electric told Sam in September they'd question paying for Pro if
  routing went to cheaper plans.
- Maya: in June we said routing stays Pro-only because it drives upgrades.
  That was before JobHarbor moved.

## Options discussed

1. Keep routing Pro-only and match JobHarbor on price instead.
2. Move routing to the Team plan and give Pro something new: multi-day jobs
   (Arc's ask), plus priority support.
3. Sell routing as an add-on to Team at $40/month.

## Decision

Option 2. Route optimization moves into the Team plan from 2027-01-01. No
price change on any plan. Pro keeps unlimited technicians and gets multi-day
jobs and priority support. Maya made the call after the discussion; Dan
agreed.

Option 3 was dropped because Sam thinks add-ons confuse small teams.

## Follow-ups

- Dan: scope multi-day jobs for Pro, aim for Q1.
- Sam: tell Pro customers before the January change, starting with Arc
  Electric.
- Raj: watch Pro downgrades and Team cancellations monthly.
- Revisit at the end of March 2027.
