## Customers
- [[field-technician]]: Technician who does the jobs and uses the mobile app on site, often with poor or no signal
- [[mid-size-contractor]]: Field service company with more than 50 technicians that needs SSO and multi-location dispatch; outside the strategy's where to play
- [[small-field-service-owner]]: Owner of a US HVAC, plumbing or electrical firm with 2 to 25 technicians who wants jobs done, invoiced and paid without paperwork

## Problems
- [[accounting-sync-duplicate-customers]]: The accounting sync creates duplicate customers that teams fix by hand
- [[long-jobs-need-multi-day-scheduling]]: Jobs that take 3 to 5 days have to be entered as one job per day with notes copied across
- [[owners-wait-too-long-to-get-paid]]: "If Crewline sent the reminders for me, I'd pay more for it": owners wait weeks for invoices to be paid and chase them by phone
- [[technicians-dont-adopt-the-mobile-app]]: Technicians at new teams never really start using the mobile app, so the office stays on paper and the team leaves in its first 90 days
- [[technicians-lose-signal-in-the-field]]: "My guys lose signal in basements and crawl spaces every day": technicians can't see or close jobs without a connection
## Features
- [[accounting-sync]]: Syncs customers and invoices with the team's accounting software; on the Team plan; sometimes creates duplicate customers
- [[android-tablet-app]]: Crewline's Android tablet app, used by fewer than 5% of technicians; left out of offline mode
- [[invoice-reminders]]: Emails the owner's customers 3, 7 and 14 days after an invoice; tested in an A/B test that missed its rule, then ordered to roll out in November
- [[multi-day-jobs]]: One job that spans several days; promised for the Pro plan, with Dan Reyes to scope it for Q1 2027
- [[offline-mode]]: Lets technicians view, update and close jobs with no signal and sync later; launched to all plans on 2026-10-01
- [[route-optimization]]: Plans technicians' routes; Pro-only today, moving into the Team plan on 2027-01-01
