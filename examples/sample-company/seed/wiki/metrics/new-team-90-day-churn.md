# New team 90-day churn

## Definition

- Measures: early cancellation of new teams
- Formula: new teams that cancel within 90 days of signup / new teams, by signup cohort; the exact cohort window isn't stated (see [[2026-06-02-strategy-memo-2026-h2]])
- Data source: unknown
- Owner: Maya Okafor (company goal)
- Timing: lagging

## Known ways it misleads

- A cohort's result is known only 90 days after it signs up.

## Baseline history

- 2026-06-02: 38% (see [[2026-06-02-strategy-memo-2026-h2]])
- 2026-07-01: 38%, the Q3 baseline (see [[2026-07-01-okrs-2026-q3]])
