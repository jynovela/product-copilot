# Weekly active technician share

## Definition

- Measures: how many paying technicians use the mobile app to do their jobs each week
- Formula: weekly active technicians / paid seats at week end, over one week, counting technicians who completed at least one job in the mobile app that week (see [[2026-07-01-okrs-2026-q3]], [[2026-10-06-metrics-weekly]])
- Data source: analytics warehouse, weekly export (see [[2026-10-06-metrics-weekly]])
- Owner: Priya Nair (Mobile goal owner)
- Timing: leading; drives [[new-team-90-day-churn]] because the strategy says teams whose technicians don't adopt the app leave (see [[2026-06-02-strategy-memo-2026-h2]])

## Known ways it misleads

- Seats grow at the same time, so the share can fall while usage grows.
- Inference: one completed job a week counts as active, so light use looks the same as daily use.

- 2026-08-03 week: 41.13% (Inference: 4,310 / 10,480) (see [[2026-10-06-metrics-weekly]])
- 2026-09-28 week: 42.66% (Inference: 4,590 / 10,760) (see [[2026-10-06-metrics-weekly]])
- 2026-10-05 week: 44.58% (Inference: 4,810 / 10,790) (see [[2026-11-17-metrics-weekly]])
- 2026-11-09 week: 46.34% (Inference: 5,060 / 10,920) (see [[2026-11-17-metrics-weekly]])
