# Technicians don't adopt the mobile app

## Problem

At many new teams, technicians never start using the mobile app, so the office keeps working from paper and phone calls, the team doesn't see the value, and it cancels within 90 days (see [[2026-06-02-strategy-memo-2026-h2]]).

## Who has it

- [[field-technician]]
- [[small-field-service-owner]]

## Evidence

- 38% of new teams cancel within 90 days; the two answers heard most are that setup takes too long and technicians don't use the mobile app (see [[2026-06-02-strategy-memo-2026-h2]])
- Weekly active technicians were 41.13% of paid seats in the week of 2026-08-03 and 46.34% in the week of 2026-11-09 (Inference: 4,310 / 10,480 and 5,060 / 10,920) (see [[2026-10-06-metrics-weekly]], [[2026-11-17-metrics-weekly]])
- Public: 27% of surveyed businesses say technicians won't use the app, the second most common problem (see [[2026-09-10-fieldops-weekly-smb-technology-survey]])
