## Product

Crewline, by Tidewater Software, schedules jobs, dispatches technicians and sends invoices for small US field service firms: HVAC, plumbing and electrical contractors (see [[2026-06-02-strategy-memo-2026-h2]]). 1,160 paying teams in the week of 2026-11-09 (see [[paying-teams]]).

- Vision: not recorded
- Product stage: growth (Inference: the strategy memo says top-of-funnel growth is fine and early churn is the problem) (see [[2026-06-02-strategy-memo-2026-h2]])

## Target customers

Owners of HVAC, plumbing and electrical firms with 2 to 22 technicians on record, on Starter, Team and Pro plans (see [[small-field-service-owner]], [[2026-09-15-crm-accounts-export]]). Technicians use the app but don't buy it (see [[field-technician]]). One prospect above 50 technicians is asking for SSO (see [[northgate-mechanical]]).

Flag: the Northgate request drifts from the strategy, which won't serve companies over 50 technicians (see [[crewline-strategy-2026-h2]]).

## Competitive position

[[jobharbor]] put offline mode on all plans on 2026-10-07 and route planning on its $119 plan on 2026-10-15; Team-plan teams have cancelled naming routing or JobHarbor (see [[2026-10-28-meeting-notes-route-optimization-pricing]]). [[fieldpilot]] won two deals on price in October (see [[2026-10-14-fieldpilot-pricing-page]]). New teams that don't adopt the app fall back to [[paper-and-phone-calls]].
