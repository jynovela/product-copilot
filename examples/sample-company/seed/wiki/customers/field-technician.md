# Field technician

## Who they are

The people who go to the job, use the Crewline mobile app to see job notes and close jobs, and record parts and photos (see [[2026-08-25-prd-offline-mode]]). They are not the buyer. Weekly active technicians were 4,310 of 10,480 paid seats in the week of 2026-08-03 and 5,060 of 10,920 in the week of 2026-11-09 (see [[2026-10-06-metrics-weekly]], [[2026-11-17-metrics-weekly]]).

## Goals

- Finish the job and close it on site, without calling the office (see [[2026-09-30-support-tickets-q3]])
