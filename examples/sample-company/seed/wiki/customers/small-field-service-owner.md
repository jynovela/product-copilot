# Small field service owner

## Who they are

Owners and office staff of US HVAC, plumbing and electrical contractors with 2 to 25 technicians, the segment the strategy targets (see [[2026-06-02-strategy-memo-2026-h2]]). Crewline had about 1,100 paying teams in June (see [[2026-06-02-strategy-memo-2026-h2]]) and 1,160 in the week of 2026-11-09 (see [[2026-11-17-metrics-weekly]]). Accounts on record range from 2 to 22 technicians (see [[2026-09-15-crm-accounts-export]]). Many owners still go out on calls themselves (see [[2026-08-12-interview-brightline-hvac]], [[2026-08-19-interview-mendez-plumbing]]).

## Goals

- Get from a booked job to a paid invoice with less office work (see [[2026-06-02-strategy-memo-2026-h2]])
- Stop chasing late payments by phone (see [[2026-08-19-interview-mendez-plumbing]])
- Have technicians record work in the app, not on paper (see [[2026-08-12-interview-brightline-hvac]])
