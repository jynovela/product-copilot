# JobHarbor

## What they offer

- 2026-10-07: offline mode on all plans: open jobs, add notes and photos and close jobs with no signal, they say (see [[2026-10-20-jobharbor-release-notes]])
- 2026-10-15: route planning included on the Crew plan, previously Business only, they say (see [[2026-10-20-jobharbor-release-notes]])
- 2026-10-15: one-click import of customers, jobs and price book from another field service app, they say (see [[2026-10-20-jobharbor-release-notes]])

## Pricing

- 2026-10-15: Crew list $119/month, up to 12 technicians, value metric technicians, free offer not shown (see [[2026-10-20-jobharbor-release-notes]])

## Strengths

- Route planning on a cheaper plan than Crewline's Pro; a Team-plan customer said JobHarbor offered it cheaper (see [[2026-09-30-support-tickets-q3]])
- 14 Team-plan teams that cancelled in September and October named routing or JobHarbor, as Sam Ortiz reported from the cancellation survey; the export wasn't attached (see [[2026-10-28-meeting-notes-route-optimization-pricing]])
- Was emailing Brightline HVAC in August (see [[2026-08-12-interview-brightline-hvac]])

None recorded.
