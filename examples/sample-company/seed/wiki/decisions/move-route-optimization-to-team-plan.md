# Move route optimization to the Team plan

- Date decided: 2026-10-28
- Decided by: Maya Okafor
- How decided (optional): decider after discussion
- Input from (optional): Dan Reyes, Sam Ortiz, Tom Becker, Raj Patel
- Reversible: at a cost
- Recorded: at the time (see [[2026-10-28-meeting-notes-route-optimization-pricing]])
- Status: active
- Replaces: [[keep-route-optimization-pro-only]]
- Reopened because (required when it replaces a decision): JobHarbor added route planning to its cheaper Crew plan on 2026-10-15, and Sam Ortiz reported 14 Team-plan cancellations naming routing or JobHarbor (see [[2026-10-28-meeting-notes-route-optimization-pricing]], [[2026-10-20-jobharbor-release-notes]])

## Decision

Route optimization moves into the Team plan from 2027-01-01. No price change on any plan. Pro keeps unlimited technicians and gets multi-day jobs and priority support (see [[2026-10-28-meeting-notes-route-optimization-pricing]]).

## Why

- JobHarbor bundles route planning in a $119 plan (see [[2026-10-20-jobharbor-release-notes]])
- 14 Team-plan teams cancelled in September and October naming routing or JobHarbor; stated by Sam Ortiz from the cancellation survey, export not attached (see [[2026-10-28-meeting-notes-route-optimization-pricing]])

- Biggest unknown: how many Pro teams downgrade once routing is on Team; 9% of Team teams upgraded to Pro in 12 months, mostly for routing (see [[2026-10-28-meeting-notes-route-optimization-pricing]])
- Existing customers: not affected on price; Pro customers to be told before January, starting with Arc Electric (see [[2026-10-28-meeting-notes-route-optimization-pricing]])

## Options not chosen

- Do nothing: keep routing Pro-only and match JobHarbor on price instead; not chosen, reason not recorded (see [[2026-10-28-meeting-notes-route-optimization-pricing]])
- Sell routing as a $40/month add-on to Team: dropped because Sam thinks add-ons confuse small teams (see [[2026-10-28-meeting-notes-route-optimization-pricing]])
