# Q3 2026 goals

- **Kind:** other
- **Evidence:** own (from the team or its customers)
- **URL:** none
- **Date of source:** 2026-07-01
- **From:** Dan Reyes, VP Product

## Summary

Dan Reyes lists the Q3 2026 goals (2026-07-01 to 2026-09-30). Company goals from Maya Okafor: net revenue retention from 96% to 101% and 90-day churn of new teams from 38% to 30%, both committed. The Mobile team (Priya Nair) has two objectives: technicians rely on Crewline in the field (launch offline mode by September 30, committed; weekly active technician share from 41% to 55%, aspirational) and make the Android tablet app great (improve the app store rating, no number). Billing (Tom Becker) aims to cut median days from invoice sent to paid from 19 to 14, committed. It defines active team as a team that scheduled at least one job in the week.

## Key points

- NRR 96% to 101%, committed, set by Maya Okafor, see [[grow-net-revenue-retention-2026-q3]]
- 90-day churn of new teams 38% to 30%, committed, see [[cut-new-team-churn-2026-q3]]
- Mobile KR1 is an output: launch offline mode by September 30, see [[technicians-rely-on-crewline-2026-q3]]
- Mobile has a second objective with no number: improve app store rating, see [[make-android-tablet-app-great-2026-q3]]
- Billing: median days invoice to paid 19 to 14, committed, see [[owners-get-paid-faster-2026-q3]]
- Active team = scheduled at least one job in the week; differs from the metrics export, see [[active-teams]]

## Quotes

- "Committed means we expect to hit it. Aspirational means we'd be happy with 70%."
