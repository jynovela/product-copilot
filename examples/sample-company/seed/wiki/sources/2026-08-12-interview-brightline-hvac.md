# Interview: Brightline HVAC

- **Kind:** interview
- **Evidence:** own (from the team or its customers)
- **URL:** none
- **Date of source:** 2026-08-12
- **From:** Owner of Brightline HVAC (8 technicians), interviewed by Priya Nair

## Summary

Priya Nair interviewed the owner of Brightline HVAC, a Team-plan customer since July 2026. Their technicians lose signal in basements and crawl spaces every day, write notes on paper and the office manager re-types them, about five hours a week of overtime. Late job closing delays invoices. The accounting sync creates duplicate customers they fix weekly. The owner said they would look elsewhere if the app doesn't work in a basement by the fall, and JobHarbor is already emailing them.
