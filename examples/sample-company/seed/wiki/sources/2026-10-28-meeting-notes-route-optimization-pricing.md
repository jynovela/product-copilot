# Meeting notes: route optimization packaging

- **Kind:** other
- **Evidence:** own (from the team or its customers)
- **URL:** none
- **Date of source:** 2026-10-28
- **From:** Leadership meeting; notes by Tom Becker

## Summary

Maya, Dan, Sam, Tom and Raj met after JobHarbor put route planning on its Crew plan. Sam said 14 Team-plan teams cancelled in September and October citing routing or JobHarbor (from the cancellation survey, not attached). Raj said 9% of Team teams upgraded to Pro in 12 months, mostly for routing. Options: keep Pro-only and cut price, move routing to Team and add multi-day jobs and priority support to Pro, or a $40 add-on. Maya chose to move routing into Team from 2027-01-01 with no price change; Dan agreed. Revisit end of March 2027.

## Key points

- Decision: route optimization in Team from 2027-01-01, no price change, see [[move-route-optimization-to-team-plan]]
- Reverses the June Pro-only decision because JobHarbor moved, see [[keep-route-optimization-pro-only]]
- 14 Team cancellations cited routing or JobHarbor (stated by Sam, export not attached), see [[jobharbor]]
- Pro gets multi-day jobs and priority support; Dan to scope multi-day jobs for Q1, see [[multi-day-jobs]]
