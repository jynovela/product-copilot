# Crewline strategy memo, H2 2026 to H1 2027

- **Kind:** other
- **Evidence:** own (from the team or its customers)
- **URL:** none
- **Date of source:** 2026-06-02
- **From:** Maya Okafor, CEO, Tidewater Software

## Summary

Maya Okafor's all-staff memo sets the strategy agreed at the 2026-05-28 offsite, owned by her and Dan Reyes. It names the challenge (38% of new teams cancel within 90 days because technicians never adopt the mobile app), where to play (US HVAC, plumbing and electrical contractors with 2 to 25 technicians) and how to win (fastest path from booked job to paid invoice). It bets on offline mode and automatic invoice reminders, rules out companies over 50 technicians, construction and non-US markets, and keeps route optimization Pro-only. The next review was due at the start of September.

## Key points

- About 1,100 paying teams, mostly US HVAC, plumbing and electrical contractors, see [[small-field-service-owner]]
- 38% of new teams cancel within their first 90 days; top reasons: setup takes too long, technicians don't use the mobile app, see [[technicians-dont-adopt-the-mobile-app]]
- Strategy choices: challenge, where to play, how to win, focus areas, bets, won't do, see [[crewline-strategy-2026-h2]]
- Route optimization stays Pro-only because it drives upgrades; decided by Maya and Dan, see [[keep-route-optimization-pro-only]]
- Won't serve companies over 50 technicians (SSO, multi-location dispatch), see [[mid-size-contractor]]

## Quotes

- "New teams don't get their technicians onto the mobile app, so they never see the value and leave in the first 90 days."
