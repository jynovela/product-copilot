# Results: automatic invoice reminders test

- **Kind:** analytics
- **Evidence:** own (from the team or its customers)
- **URL:** none
- **Date of source:** 2026-10-20
- **From:** Raj Patel, Analyst

## Summary

Control (412 teams) median 19.0 days, treatment (409 teams) 17.2, a 1.8-day difference with p = 0.08. Complaint rate 0.4%. 39% of treatment teams turned reminders off at some point; Starter teams improved more than Team teams (2.6 vs 1.1 days) but the test wasn't sized for that; 4 weeks may be too short. The result didn't meet the ship threshold.

## Key points

- 1.8 days lower, p = 0.08: below the rule's 3 days and p < 0.05, see [[invoice-reminders-ab-test]]
- Complaint rate 0.4%, within the 1% guardrail, see [[reminder-complaint-rate]]
- 39% of treatment teams turned reminders off at some point, see [[invoice-reminders]]
