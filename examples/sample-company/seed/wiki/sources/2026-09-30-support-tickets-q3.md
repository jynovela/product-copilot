# Support tickets, Q3 2026

- **Kind:** other
- **Evidence:** own (from the team or its customers)
- **URL:** none
- **Date of source:** 2026-09-30
- **From:** Lena Park, Support lead

## Summary

Q3 2026 tickets by tag: billing-question 230, offline-sync 212, login 140, invoice-sync 96, android-tablet 61, routing 18, other 483, total 1,240. Ten example tickets, picked by Lena, not random. Lena estimates maybe a third of billing questions are about chasing unpaid invoices, untagged. Offline-sync tickets dropped after the beta started, but beta teams were chosen from complainers, so that drop isn't a fair comparison. One example ticket contains a credential-like string, left out of the wiki.
