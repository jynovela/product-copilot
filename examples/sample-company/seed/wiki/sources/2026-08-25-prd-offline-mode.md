# PRD: Offline mode for the technician app

- **Kind:** PRD
- **Evidence:** own (from the team or its customers)
- **URL:** none
- **Date of source:** 2026-08-25
- **From:** Priya Nair, Senior PM, Mobile

## Summary

Priya Nair's PRD, approved for build by Dan Reyes on 2026-08-27, scopes offline mode: view jobs, add notes and photos, mark parts, close jobs and capture signatures offline, syncing later. Out: offline job creation, schedule editing and the Android tablet app (under 5% of technicians). Success: weekly offline sync errors down at least 50% from the August average within 6 weeks of general availability (failure under 20%); weekly active technician share up 5 points from 41% within 8 weeks; crash rate must stay within 0.5% of sessions. Launch: tier 1, invite-only beta from 2026-09-15 with 50 teams, GA planned 2026-10-01 on all plans.

## Key points

- Scope in, out and first slice; approved for build by Dan Reyes on 2026-08-27, see [[approve-offline-mode-scope]]
- Success metrics and crash guardrail, see [[offline-mode]]
- Claims more than 300 offline tickets a quarter, citing support; the support export shows 212 in Q3, see [[technicians-lose-signal-in-the-field]]
- Android tablet app used by fewer than 5% of technicians, see [[android-tablet-app]]
