# Interview: Mendez Plumbing

- **Kind:** interview
- **Evidence:** own (from the team or its customers)
- **URL:** none
- **Date of source:** 2026-08-19
- **From:** Owner of Mendez Plumbing (3 technicians), interviewed by Tom Becker

## Summary

Tom Becker interviewed the owner of Mendez Plumbing, a Starter-plan customer since March 2025. He has never had an offline problem because they work above ground in houses. His real pain is getting paid: about a third of invoices sit unpaid over 30 days, he built his own spreadsheet to track them, and his wife phones late payers every Friday. He said he'd pay more for automatic reminders sent from the business, not his phone.

- Built a spreadsheet to track unpaid invoices; calls late payers weekly, see [[owners-wait-too-long-to-get-paid]]
