# FieldOps Weekly 2026 Field Service Technology Survey

- **Kind:** survey
- **Evidence:** public (from the web)
- **URL:** <https://fieldopsweekly.example/2026-smb-technology-survey>
- **Date of source:** 2026-09-10
- **From:** FieldOps Weekly

## Summary

A survey of 412 US field service businesses with 1 to 50 technicians, sponsored by an unnamed payments company, with no method or margin of error given. It says 64% still use paper work orders for some jobs, 31% name poor connectivity as the top mobile app problem, 27% say technicians won't use the app, the median business waits 22 days to be paid, and 48% would pay more to get paid faster. Public evidence about the market, not about Crewline's customers.
