- Adopted: 2026-05-28 by Maya Okafor and Dan Reyes (see [[adopt-crewline-strategy-2026-h2]])

## Challenge

New teams don't get their technicians onto the mobile app, so they never see the value and leave in their first 90 days; 38% of new teams cancel within 90 days. (see [[technicians-dont-adopt-the-mobile-app]], [[2026-06-02-strategy-memo-2026-h2]])

- Market size: unknown
  - Basis: unknown
- Not serving: companies over 50 technicians, construction project management, markets outside the US in 2026 (see [[2026-06-02-strategy-memo-2026-h2]])

## How to win

- Why customers pick us over [[paper-and-phone-calls]] or doing nothing: the fastest path from a booked job to a paid invoice, with a mobile app technicians actually use (see [[2026-06-02-strategy-memo-2026-h2]])
- Differentiators: not listed beyond the line above
- Advantage it rests on: none yet
- Business benefit: retention (unmeasured here; measured goals point to this page)

## Focus areas

- Approach: get technicians onto the app and shorten time to payment (see [[2026-06-02-strategy-memo-2026-h2]])
- Technician adoption of the mobile app: the challenge (see [[technicians-dont-adopt-the-mobile-app]])
- Getting paid, from invoice sent to money in the bank (see [[owners-wait-too-long-to-get-paid]])

## Bets

- Offline mode: we believe technicians can then work in basements, crawl spaces and rural areas without signal; it's wrong if weekly active technician share doesn't rise; checked by [[weekly-active-technician-share]]. The memo gives no date or signal; the signal here is Inference.
- Automatic invoice reminders: we believe owners stop chasing payments by phone; it's wrong if days to paid don't fall; checked by [[invoice-reminders-ab-test]]

## Won't do

- Companies with more than 50 technicians: they need SSO, multi-location dispatch and custom contracts, and pull the roadmap away from small teams (see [[2026-06-02-strategy-memo-2026-h2]])
- Construction project management (see [[2026-06-02-strategy-memo-2026-h2]])
- Markets outside the US in 2026 (see [[2026-06-02-strategy-memo-2026-h2]])
