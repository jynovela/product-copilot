# Do Things That Don't Scale

- **Author:** Paul Graham (2013)
- **Topics:** [[startups-and-business-building]]
- **On the list:** [[recommended-reading-for-product-owners]]

## Why it's on the list

- The product owner reading list lists it under "Recommended reading". Recommended as a warning against scaling or "big launching" too early. (see [[recommended-reading-for-product-owners]])

## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the essay. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Startups take off because founders recruit users manually and delight them in ways that don't scale. See [[build-measure-learn]].
- Examples: Airbnb's founders photographing listings themselves; Stripe's founders installing it for users on the spot.
- Don't build for scale before you know you solve a real problem. See [[invisible-asymptote]].
