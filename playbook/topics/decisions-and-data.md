# Decisions and data

## What this covers

Cognitive biases, decision quality versus outcomes, statistics traps, and goal-setting with metrics.

## Core ideas

General knowledge: a synthesis of the books below, from model memory.

- Judge decisions by the process and information available, not only by how they turned out.
- Our fast intuitive thinking is biased; slow down for important calls.
- Metrics can mislead; check samples, averages, charts and causation.

## Concepts

- [[resulting]]: Judging a decision by its outcome instead of by the decision process.
- [[premortem]]: Imagine the project failed, then explain why, before you start.
- [[system-1-and-system-2]]: Fast, intuitive thinking versus slow, deliberate thinking, and the biases that come from the first.
- [[statistical-traps]]: Common ways numbers mislead: biased samples, chosen averages, misleading charts, false causation.
- [[okrs]]: A goal (objective) plus a few measurable results that show progress toward it.

## Questions to ask about your product

- What would make this decision wrong?
- What's the sample behind this metric?
- Are we judging the last launch by luck or by decision quality?
