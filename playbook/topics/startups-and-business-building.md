# Startups and business building

## What this covers

How new products and companies find fit, defend themselves, get funded and survive crises.

## Core ideas

General knowledge: a synthesis of the books below, from model memory.

- Treat a new product as a set of hypotheses to test quickly.
- Aim for a differentiated position, not head-to-head competition.
- Growth that outruns cash or fit kills companies.

- What would stop growth in a year (our invisible asymptote)?
