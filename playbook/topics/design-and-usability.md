# Design and usability

## What this covers

Principles of usable design and lightweight ways to check usability.

## Core ideas

General knowledge: a synthesis of the books below, from model memory.

- Users shouldn't have to think about how to use something; make actions obvious.
- Most user error is design error.
- Test with a handful of real users, often.

## Concepts

- [[affordances-and-signifiers]]: Design that makes possible actions obvious and gives clear feedback.

## Questions to ask about your product

- Where do users get stuck or make mistakes?
- When did we last watch someone use the product?
