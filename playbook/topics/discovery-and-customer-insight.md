# Discovery and customer insight

## What this covers

How to learn about customers, frame their problems, and test ideas cheaply before committing engineering time.

## Core ideas

General knowledge: a synthesis of the books below, from model memory.

- Talk to customers continuously, as a team, not in occasional big research projects.
- Frame needs as the progress customers want in a situation (jobs), not as feature requests.
- Test the riskiest assumptions first with prototypes and small experiments.

## Questions to ask about your product

- Who did we last talk to, and when?
- Which customer problem is this feature for, and what's the evidence?
- What's the riskiest assumption in our current plan?
