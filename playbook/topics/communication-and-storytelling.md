## What this covers

Clear writing, memorable messages, storytelling and listening.

## Core ideas

General knowledge: a synthesis of the books below, from model memory.

- Find the core message and make it concrete.
- Stories with a character facing change hold attention.
- Good writing is mostly cutting.
