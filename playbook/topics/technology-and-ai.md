# Technology and AI

## What this covers

Technology basics for non-engineers, AI textbooks and AI's effect on business and competition.

## Core ideas

General knowledge: a synthesis of the books below, from model memory.

- PMs don't need to code, but they need enough fluency to judge feasibility and trade-offs.
- AI changes how products are built and how companies compete.
- Deep technical textbooks are optional reference, not required reading.
