# PM career and skills

## What this covers

Interview preparation, introductory handbooks, technical fluency for PMs, and career growth.

## Core ideas

General knowledge: a synthesis of the books below, from model memory.

- The PM role varies by company; learn what each one expects.
- Practice structured thinking for interviews, but show real product judgment.
- Build technical and business fluency alongside product skills.
