# Self-leadership and productivity

## What this covers

Self-awareness, adult development, handling the inner voice, and personal workflow.

## Core ideas

General knowledge: a synthesis of the books below, from model memory.

- Notice when you're reacting from fear or defensiveness and shift to curiosity.
- Lasting change often needs uncovering hidden competing commitments.
- A trusted system for tasks frees attention for thinking.

## Concepts

- [[reactive-vs-creative-leadership]]: Leading from fear and defensiveness versus from purpose, curiosity and responsibility.
- [[immunity-map]]: A four-column map that reveals the hidden commitments blocking a change.
- [[distanced-self-talk]]: Talk to yourself by name or as "you" to calm the inner critic and think clearly.
- [[gtd-workflow]]: Capture, clarify, organize, reflect, engage: a trusted system for everything on your plate.
- [[habit-design]]: Make good behaviors obvious, attractive, easy and satisfying.

## Questions to ask about your product

- Where am I acting from fear of being wrong?
- What change do I keep failing to make, and what am I protecting?
