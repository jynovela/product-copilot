# Newsletters and podcasts

A light listing, not a knowledge base: these change every week, so there are no ideas to extract. Names come from [[top-pm-books-newsletters-podcasts]]. Its descriptions were not reliable, so they are not used. General knowledge: the notes below are mine; where I don't know a resource well, there is no note.

## Newsletters

- **Lenny's Newsletter** (Lenny Rachitsky): Product, growth and career advice; one of the most widely read PM newsletters.
- **The Beautiful Mess** (John Cutler): Product and organizational systems: how product teams and companies actually work. The source list describes it as marketing and personal brand, which is wrong.
- **Andrew Chen's newsletter** (Andrew Chen): Growth, network effects and consumer tech, from an a16z partner.
- **Benedict Evans** (Benedict Evans): Analysis of technology trends and their business impact.
- **Elena's Growth Scoop** (Elena Verna): Product-led growth and B2B growth strategy.
- **Growth Unhinged** (Kyle Poyar): SaaS growth, pricing and product-led growth, often data-driven.
- **Growth.Design case studies** (Growth.Design): Comic-style case studies on UX and product psychology.
- **First Round Review** (First Round Capital): In-depth tactical articles from startup operators.
- **Product Habits** (Hiten Shah): SaaS product, positioning and customer research.
- **Category Pirates** (Eddie Yoon, Christopher Lochhead and Katrina Kirsch): Category design: creating and owning new market categories.
- **ByteByteGo** (Alex Xu): Visual explanations of system design; useful for technical fluency.
- **Demand Curve** (Demand Curve): Growth marketing tactics. The list credits Julian Shapiro; I'm not sure he runs it today.
- **UX Collective** (UX Collective): Community publication of UX design articles.
- **User Interviews** (User Interviews): User research methods and practice, from a research recruiting company.
- **UX Booth**
- **Product Managers at Work**
- **Leah's ProducTea** (Leah Tharin): Product-led growth and product strategy.
- **The Hard Parts of Growth** (Ami Vora)
- **The India Notes**
- **Andy Fishman's newsletter** (Andy Fishman)
- **Sidebar**: Five curated design links each weekday.

## Podcasts

- **Lenny's Podcast** (Lenny Rachitsky): Long interviews with product and growth leaders.
- **Masters of Scale** (Reid Hoffman): Founder interviews about scaling companies.
- **How I Built This** (Guy Raz): Founder origin stories.
- **UX Podcast** (James Royal-Lawson and Per Axbom): Long-running podcast on UX and digital design.
- **The Design MBA Show** (Jayneil Dalal)
- **UX Bites**
- **AI in Business** (Daniel Faggella)
- **In Machines We Trust** (MIT Technology Review)
- **AI Today** (Kathleen Walch and Ron Schmelzer)
- **The LaunchNotes Podcast**
