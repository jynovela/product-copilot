# The Innovator's Dilemma

- **Author:** Clayton M. Christensen
- **Topics:** [[strategy-and-innovation]]
- **On the list:** [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]], marked as a top pick

## Why it's on the list

To Ken, the most important business and product book of the past fifty years. Technology PMs should read it now. (see [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]])

## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Well-run companies fail precisely because they listen to their best customers and pursue higher margins. See [[disruptive-innovation]].
- Sustaining innovations improve products for existing customers; disruptive ones start simpler, cheaper and worse, in overlooked markets, then improve. See [[disruptive-innovation]].
- Incumbents overshoot what mainstream customers need, leaving room below. See [[disruptive-innovation]].
- To pursue disruption, incumbents often need a separate unit sized to the small new market. See [[disruptive-innovation]].

## Use it when

- Assessing a low-end competitor
- Deciding whether to build a cheaper, simpler product
