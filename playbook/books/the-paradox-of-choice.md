## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

Confidence: lower. Some later studies of the "too much choice" effect found it smaller or context-dependent.

- More options can lead to decision paralysis and lower satisfaction with the choice made. See [[choice-architecture]].
- Maximizers (seek the best) suffer more than satisficers (seek good enough).
- Regret and opportunity costs grow with the number of options.
