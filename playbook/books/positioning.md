# Positioning: The Battle for Your Mind

- **Author:** Al Ries and Jack Trout
- **Topics:** [[positioning-and-go-to-market]]
- **On the list:** [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]]

## Why it's on the list

The classic marketing book and one of the first about positioning a product in the customer's mind. (see [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]])

## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Positioning happens in the prospect's mind, not in the product. See [[positioning-in-the-mind]].
- It's better to be first in a category than better; if you can't be first, create a new category. See [[positioning-in-the-mind]].
- Position against the leader (e.g. Avis's "We try harder") rather than ignore it. See [[positioning-in-the-mind]].
- Line extensions dilute a strong position. See [[positioning-in-the-mind]].

## Use it when

- Naming and framing a new product
- Competitive messaging
