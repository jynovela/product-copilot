## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Start a product by writing the press release and FAQ (PR/FAQ) from the customer's view. See [[working-backwards-pr-faq]].
- Replace slide decks with six-page narrative memos read silently at the start of meetings. See [[working-backwards-pr-faq]].
- Single-threaded leaders own one initiative full time.
- The bar raiser process keeps hiring standards high.
- Manage controllable input metrics, not just output metrics. See [[statistical-traps]].
