# The 15 Commitments of Conscious Leadership

- **Author:** Jim Dethmer, Diana Chapman and Kaley Warner Klemp
- **Topics:** [[self-leadership-and-productivity]], [[leadership-and-management]]
- **On the list:** [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]], marked as a top pick

## Why it's on the list

The book that changed Ken most as a leader and person. His one pick for the human skills behind product management. (see [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]])

## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Notice whether you are "above the line" (open, curious, committed to learning) or "below the line" (closed, defensive, committed to being right). See [[reactive-vs-creative-leadership]].
- Take full responsibility instead of blaming, and choose curiosity over being right. See [[reactive-vs-creative-leadership]].
- Speak candidly, stop gossip, and keep agreements (integrity).
- Work in your zone of genius, and see the world as an ally rather than a threat.

- Conflict or blame on the team
- Building self-awareness as a leader
