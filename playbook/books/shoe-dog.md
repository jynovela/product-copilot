## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Nike started as Blue Ribbon Sports, importing Japanese running shoes.
- Growth repeatedly outran cash; the company survived crisis after crisis.
- A small, loyal, unconventional team and persistence carried it.
