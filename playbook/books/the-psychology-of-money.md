## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Financial success depends more on behavior than on knowledge.
- Compounding and staying in the game matter most; leave room for error.
- Luck and risk shape outcomes; rare tail events drive most results. See [[resulting]].
- Knowing when you have "enough" prevents ruinous risks.
