## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- The PM role differs a lot between companies; learn what each one expects.
- Prepare for the main question types: product design, estimation, strategy, technical and behavioral. See [[circles-method]].
- Show product sense, structured thinking and evidence of shipping.
