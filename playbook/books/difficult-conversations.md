## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Every difficult conversation is really three: what happened, feelings, and identity (what it says about me). See [[three-conversations]].
- Separate intent from impact: you don't know their intent, and your impact may not match yours. See [[three-conversations]].
- Replace blame with contribution: map how each person contributed to the situation. See [[three-conversations]].
- Start from the "third story", the version a neutral observer would tell, and aim for a learning conversation. See [[three-conversations]].
