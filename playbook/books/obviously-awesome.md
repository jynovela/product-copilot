## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Positioning defines how your product is the best at something a defined market cares about. See [[positioning-in-the-mind]].
- Components: competitive alternatives, unique attributes, value, best-fit customers, market category and relevant trends. See [[positioning-in-the-mind]].
- Start from what customers would use if you didn't exist, then work out what's unique. See [[jobs-to-be-done]].
