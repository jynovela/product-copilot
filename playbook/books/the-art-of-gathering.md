## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Give every gathering a specific, disputable purpose, not a generic category.
- Hosts should use their authority generously, not be "chill".
- Decide who to leave out, and open and close the gathering deliberately.

## Use it when

- Planning workshops, offsites and kickoffs
- Fixing pointless meetings
