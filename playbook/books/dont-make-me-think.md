- Ry Sullivan also lists it, under "Designing products". (see [[2025-03-06-ry-sullivan-pm-reading-list]])

## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Pages and screens should be self-evident; every question a user has to think about adds friction. See [[affordances-and-signifiers]].
- Users scan, pick the first reasonable option, and muddle through rather than read instructions.
- Follow conventions, make clickable things obvious, and cut needless words. See [[affordances-and-signifiers]].
- Do cheap usability testing often: a few users, regularly, catches most big problems. See [[four-product-risks]].

## Use it when

- Reviewing a design or flow
- Setting up lightweight usability tests
