## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Talk about their life, not your idea. See [[mom-test-interviews]].
- Ask about specifics in the past, not opinions or the future. See [[mom-test-interviews]].
- Talk less, listen more. Compliments are worthless; look for commitment and advancement instead. See [[mom-test-interviews]].
