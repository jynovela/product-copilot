## Core argument

Customer conversations go wrong by default, because people lie to be kind
and founders hear what they want. Fix it by asking about people's lives and
past behavior instead of your idea, treating compliments and promises as
noise, and judging interest only by what people give up. (see [[2013-08-01-rob-fitzpatrick-the-mom-test]])

## What it adds

- The most concrete question-level rules of any discovery book on the list,
  with a bank of good and bad questions and how to fix each bad one.
- A test for meetings: a meeting succeeds only with a commitment of time,
  reputation or money.
- A light process for small teams, from prep to signal-coded notes.

Weaker on: sample design, synthesis across many interviews, and remote
research (written in 2013, before remote interviews were common).
