## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Five dysfunctions build on each other: absence of trust, fear of conflict, lack of commitment, avoidance of accountability, inattention to results. See [[five-dysfunctions]].
- Trust here means vulnerability-based trust, which makes healthy conflict possible. See [[five-dysfunctions]].
