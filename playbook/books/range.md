## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- In kind learning environments (clear rules, fast feedback) early specialization works; in wicked ones it doesn't.
- A sampling period before specializing improves match quality.
- Analogical thinking across domains helps solve novel problems.
