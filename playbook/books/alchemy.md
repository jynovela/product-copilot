## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- People act on "psycho-logic", not pure logic; perceived value is real value. See [[persuasion-principles]].
- Cheap changes in perception can beat expensive engineering (make the wait feel shorter instead of making it shorter). See [[choice-architecture]].
- The opposite of a good idea can also be a good idea; test counterintuitive options.
- Costly signals build trust.

## Use it when

- Improving perceived quality or speed
- Brainstorming counterintuitive solutions
