## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Four villains of decisions: narrow framing, confirmation bias, short-term emotion and overconfidence. See [[wrap-decision-process]].
- WRAP: Widen your options, Reality-test your assumptions, Attain distance before deciding, Prepare to be wrong. See [[wrap-decision-process]].
- Tools include the vanishing options test, 10/10/10 and small experiments ("ooching"). See [[wrap-decision-process]].

## Use it when

- Big product or career decisions
- Team decisions stuck in either/or framing
