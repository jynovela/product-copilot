# Thanks for the Feedback: The Science and Art of Receiving Feedback Well

- **Author:** Douglas Stone and Sheila Heen
- **Topics:** [[negotiation-feedback-and-relationships]]
- **On the list:** [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]]

## Why it's on the list

- PMs must give and take feedback candidly and kindly. Another Harvard Negotiation Project book that teaches how. (see [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]])

## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Feedback comes in three kinds: appreciation, coaching and evaluation; mix-ups cause conflict. See [[feedback-triggers]].
- Three triggers block us: truth (it's wrong), relationship (who's saying it) and identity (what it says about me). See [[feedback-triggers]].
- We all have blind spots; ask others what they see that you don't. See [[feedback-triggers]].

## Use it when

- After a tough review
- Asking your team for feedback
