## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Finite games have fixed rules and an end; business has no end, so the goal is to keep playing.
- Leaders need a just cause, trusting teams, a worthy rival, existential flexibility and the courage to lead.
