## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Five forces shape industry profitability: rivalry, buyers, suppliers, substitutes and new entrants. See [[five-forces]].
- Operational effectiveness (doing the same things better) is not strategy; strategy is being different. See [[five-forces]].
- Competitive advantage shows up in the value chain, through trade-offs, fit between activities and continuity. See [[five-forces]].
- Aim to be unique, not best. See [[positioning-in-the-mind]].
