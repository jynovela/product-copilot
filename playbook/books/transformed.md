# Transformed: Moving to the Product Operating Model

- **Author:** Marty Cagan with Lea Hickman, Chris Jones, Christian Idiodi and John Moore
- **Topics:** [[product-teams-and-operating-model]], [[leadership-and-management]]
- **On the list:** [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]]

## Why it's on the list

The third book in Cagan's trilogy: moving a company from project to product culture. More for executives than ICs, but hopeful for anyone stuck on a feature team. (see [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]])

## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- The product operating model changes how you build (small, frequent, reliable releases), how you solve problems (empowered teams) and how you decide which problems to solve (product strategy). See [[product-operating-model]].
- It needs new competencies (strong product managers, designers, tech leads, leaders) and new principles. See [[product-operating-model]], [[empowered-product-teams]].
- Transformation needs executive sponsorship and is mostly about changing culture. See [[product-operating-model]].
