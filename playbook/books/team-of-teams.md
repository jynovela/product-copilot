## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Complex environments can't be managed by efficiency and command alone; adaptability matters more.
- Shared consciousness: radical transparency so every team understands the whole. See [[empowered-product-teams]].
- Empowered execution: push decisions to the people closest to the problem. See [[empowered-product-teams]].

## Use it when

- Coordinating many teams
- Moving decisions closer to the work
