# Continuous Discovery Habits

- **Author:** Teresa Torres
- **Topics:** [[discovery-and-customer-insight]], [[product-teams-and-operating-model]]
- **On the list:** [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]] (Ken Norton top pick)

## Why it's on the list

- Torres is among the best at helping teams build what customers want. The book shows how to turn product work into continuous discovery and learning. (see [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]])

## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- The team that builds the product talks to customers at least weekly, in small research activities, in pursuit of a desired outcome. See [[product-trio]].
- A product trio (product manager, designer, tech lead) does discovery together. See [[product-trio]].
- Map the path from a desired outcome to opportunities (customer needs, pains, desires) to solutions to assumption tests. See [[opportunity-solution-tree]].
- Interview for specific past stories ("tell me about the last time..."), not opinions or predictions. See [[opportunity-solution-tree]].
- Compare several solutions for the same opportunity and test the riskiest assumptions with small, fast tests. See [[four-product-risks]].

## Use it when

- Setting up a regular customer interview cadence
- Choosing which problem to work on next
- Turning interviews into decisions
