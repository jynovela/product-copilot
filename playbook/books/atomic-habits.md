## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Small improvements compound; focus on systems rather than goals. See [[habit-design]].
- Four laws: make it obvious, attractive, easy and satisfying (invert them to break habits). See [[habit-design]].
- Identity-based habits: change who you believe you are, not just what you do. See [[habit-design]].
