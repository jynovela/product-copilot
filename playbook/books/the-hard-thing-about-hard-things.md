## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- There is no recipe for hard problems; leaders live in "the struggle".
- Wartime and peacetime leadership need different styles.
- Take care of the people, the products and the profits, in that order.
- Includes the "Good Product Manager/Bad Product Manager" memo on what strong PMs do.

- Leading through layoffs, crises or pivots
- Defining PM expectations
