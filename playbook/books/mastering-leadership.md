# Mastering Leadership

- **Author:** Robert J. Anderson and William A. Adams
- **Topics:** [[self-leadership-and-productivity]], [[leadership-and-management]]
- **On the list:** [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]]

## Why it's on the list

A developmental path for leaders with a stage model of leadership. The shift from Reactive to Creative leadership matters most for product leaders. (see [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]])

## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Leadership effectiveness grows as leaders move through stages, mainly from Reactive to Creative. See [[reactive-vs-creative-leadership]].
- Reactive leaders are driven by external expectations (complying, protecting, controlling); Creative leaders act from purpose and vision. See [[reactive-vs-creative-leadership]].
- The Universal Model of Leadership and the Leadership Circle Profile measure these patterns. See [[reactive-vs-creative-leadership]].

- Moving from IC PM into product leadership
- 360 feedback and coaching
