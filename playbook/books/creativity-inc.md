## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- The Braintrust: a group of peers gives candid notes on work in progress, with no power to mandate changes. See [[braintrust]].
- Early ideas are "ugly babies": protect new work from being judged against finished products. See [[braintrust]].
- Candor beats politeness; build places where people can say what they really think.
- Failure is a necessary cost of doing new things; leaders should reduce the fear of it, not the fact of it.

## Use it when

- Running design or product reviews
- Building a culture where people raise problems early
