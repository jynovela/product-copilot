# Thinking in Bets: Making Smarter Decisions When You Don't Have All the Facts

- **Author:** Annie Duke
- **Topics:** [[decisions-and-data]]
- **On the list:** [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]]

## Why it's on the list

PMs make many decisions daily with incomplete information. A poker champion turned decision researcher teaches comfort with uncertainty. (see [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]])

## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Resulting: judging a decision by its outcome. Good decisions can have bad outcomes because of luck. See [[resulting]].
- Treat decisions as bets; state beliefs with confidence levels and ask "wanna bet?" See [[resulting]].
- Build a truth-seeking group that rewards accuracy over agreement. See [[resulting]].
- Imagine futures: backcast from success and run premortems from failure. See [[premortem]].
