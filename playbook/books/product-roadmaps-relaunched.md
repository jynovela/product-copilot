## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- A roadmap is a statement of intent and direction, not a release plan of dated features. See [[build-trap]].
- Organize roadmaps around themes: customer problems or outcomes, linked to vision and objectives. See [[build-trap]], [[okrs]].
- Use the roadmap to align stakeholders, and say no with a clear rationale. See [[positive-no]].
