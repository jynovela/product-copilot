## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Tactical empathy: understand and name the other side's feelings. See [[tactical-empathy]].
- Mirroring and labeling ("it seems like...") get people talking. See [[tactical-empathy]].
- Calibrated "how" and "what" questions make the other side solve your problem. See [[tactical-empathy]].
- Aim for "that's right", not "you're right"; start with an accusation audit. See [[tactical-empathy]].
