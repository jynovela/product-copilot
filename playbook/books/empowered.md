# Empowered: Ordinary People, Extraordinary Products

- **Author:** Marty Cagan with Chris Jones
- **Topics:** [[product-teams-and-operating-model]], [[leadership-and-management]], [[strategy-and-innovation]]
- **On the list:** [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]] (Ken Norton top pick)

## Why it's on the list

- The sequel to Inspired. It argues the best product companies win less by hiring better and more by how they enable people to work together on hard problems. (see [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]])

## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Feature teams are handed solutions to build; empowered teams are given problems to solve and are accountable for outcomes. See [[empowered-product-teams]].
- Leaders provide context (vision, strategy, objectives) and coach; they don't dictate solutions. See [[empowered-product-teams]].
- Product strategy is about focus, insights, turning insights into action, and active management of the work. See [[empowered-product-teams]].
- Coaching product people is a manager's most important job. See [[one-on-ones]].

## Use it when

- Arguing for outcome-based roadmaps
- Setting up team objectives
- Coaching PMs
