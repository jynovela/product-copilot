## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Customers "hire" products to make progress in a specific circumstance; they "fire" them when something does the job better. See [[jobs-to-be-done]].
- Jobs have functional, social and emotional dimensions; the circumstance matters more than customer demographics. See [[jobs-to-be-done]].
- Famous example: a milkshake hired for a long, boring morning commute competes with bagels and bananas, not other milkshakes. See [[jobs-to-be-done]].
- Understanding the job turns innovation from luck into something more predictable. See [[disruptive-innovation]].
