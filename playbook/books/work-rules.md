## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Hire slowly and well: structured interviews and work samples predict performance better than gut feel.
- Give people more freedom than you're comfortable giving. See [[empowered-product-teams]].
- Use data to make people decisions and test changes like experiments.
- Performance is uneven; reward the best people accordingly.
