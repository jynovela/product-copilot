## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- People rely on mental shortcuts when deciding to comply; persuaders trigger them. See [[persuasion-principles]].
- The classic principles: reciprocity, commitment and consistency, social proof, liking, authority and scarcity. Later editions add unity (shared identity). See [[persuasion-principles]].
- The same principles can be used ethically (honest social proof) or manipulatively (fake scarcity). See [[persuasion-principles]].
