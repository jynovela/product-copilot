## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Google's culture leaned on data and engineering judgment over opinion and hierarchy. See [[resulting]].
- Launch early and iterate, guided by user metrics.
- Ambitious goals and room for side projects were used to drive innovation.
- The Associate Product Manager program grew PMs early in their careers.
