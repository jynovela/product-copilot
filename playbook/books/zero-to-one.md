## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Going from 0 to 1 (new) is different from 1 to n (copying what works).
- Competition erodes profits; great businesses build a monopoly in a small market and expand. See [[positioning-in-the-mind]].
- Look for secrets: important truths few people agree with.
- Monopoly traits: proprietary technology, network effects, economies of scale, branding.

- Choosing a first market
