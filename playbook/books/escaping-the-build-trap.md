# Escaping the Build Trap

- **Author:** Melissa Perri
- **Topics:** [[product-teams-and-operating-model]], [[strategy-and-innovation]]
- **On the list:** [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]]

## Why it's on the list

How solid product management helps companies solve real customer problems and hit business goals, and how to build a product culture that serves both. (see [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]])

## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- The build trap: measuring success by output (features shipped) instead of outcomes (value created). See [[build-trap]].
- Value is an exchange: customers get problems solved, the business gets revenue or other value back. See [[build-trap]].
- The PM is neither a mini-CEO nor a waiter taking orders; the role is to connect business goals to customer problems.
- Use a product kata: understand the direction, the current state, the next target condition, then experiment toward it. See [[okrs]].

## Use it when

- When the roadmap is a list of features with no goals
- Explaining the PM role to a company
