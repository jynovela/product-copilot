# The Captain Class: The Hidden Force That Creates the World's Greatest Teams

- **Author:** Sam Walker
- **Topics:** [[leadership-and-management]]
- **On the list:** [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]]

## Why it's on the list

- The founding editor of the Wall Street Journal's sports section studies the captains of history's best sports teams. Their qualities are not what you'd expect. (see [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]])

## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- The most dominant teams shared a particular kind of captain, more than a star player or coach.
- Those captains were relentless, did thankless work, communicated practically, and held strong emotional control.
- They were often not the most talented player and were willing to stand apart and challenge authority.

## Use it when

- Thinking about what leadership looks like inside a team
- Choosing team leads
