If you could read only one product management book, Ken says this is it. (see [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]])

## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Address four risks early: value, usability, feasibility and business viability. See [[four-product-risks]].
- Separate discovery (figuring out what to build) from delivery (building it well); do discovery continuously. See [[four-product-risks]], [[opportunity-solution-tree]].
- Use prototypes to test ideas quickly and cheaply before committing engineering effort. See [[design-sprint]].
- Strong PMs know the customer, the data, the business and the market deeply.
- Teams should be missionaries, not mercenaries: empowered and accountable for outcomes. See [[empowered-product-teams]].
