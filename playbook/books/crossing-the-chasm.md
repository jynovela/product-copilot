## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Technology adoption moves through innovators, early adopters, early majority, late majority and laggards. See [[technology-adoption-lifecycle]].
- There is a chasm between early adopters (visionaries who want change) and the early majority (pragmatists who want proven solutions and references). See [[technology-adoption-lifecycle]].
- Cross it by dominating one narrow beachhead segment and delivering the whole product it needs. See [[technology-adoption-lifecycle]].
- Use a clear positioning statement: for whom, which need, what category, key benefit, versus which alternative. See [[positioning-in-the-mind]].
