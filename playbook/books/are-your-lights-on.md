## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- A problem is a difference between things as desired and things as perceived.
- Ask what the problem is, whose problem it is, where it comes from, and whether we really want to solve it. See [[opportunity-solution-tree]].
- Each solution creates new problems; check before committing.
