## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Loonshots are dismissed early ideas; franchises are proven businesses. Both are needed. See [[loonshots-and-franchises]].
- Phase separation: give loonshot teams and franchise teams different homes and incentives. See [[loonshots-and-franchises]], [[disruptive-innovation]].
- Keep a dynamic balance: leaders manage the transfer between the two, rather than judging ideas themselves. See [[loonshots-and-franchises]].
- The Moses trap: when a leader picks loonshots alone, the flow eventually breaks. See [[loonshots-and-franchises]].
