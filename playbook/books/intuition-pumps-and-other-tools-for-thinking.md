## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Intuition pumps are thought experiments; check how each knob changes the conclusion.
- Rapoport's rules: restate the other view fairly, note agreement and what you learned, then criticize. See [[principled-negotiation]].
- Sturgeon's law: 90 percent of everything is crud, so judge a field by its best work.
