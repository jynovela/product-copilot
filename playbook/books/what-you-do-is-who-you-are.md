# What You Do Is Who You Are

- **Author:** Ben Horowitz

## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Culture is how people behave when you're not watching; actions define it, not stated values.
- Uses historical examples, such as Toussaint Louverture and the samurai, to show how leaders shape culture.
- Shocking rules and leaders' own behavior make cultural values stick.

## Use it when

- Shaping team culture
- When stated values don't match behavior
