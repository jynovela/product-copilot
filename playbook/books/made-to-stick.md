## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Sticky ideas are Simple, Unexpected, Concrete, Credible, Emotional, and told as Stories (SUCCESs). See [[sticky-ideas-success]].
- The curse of knowledge: once you know something, it's hard to imagine not knowing it, so experts explain badly. See [[sticky-ideas-success]].
- Find the core: a commander's-intent style statement of what matters most. See [[sticky-ideas-success]].
