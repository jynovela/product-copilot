## Why it's on the list

From Ken's former GV partners: how to cut through noise, pick a problem and test a solution in one week. (see [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]])

## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- A five-day process: map the problem, sketch solutions, decide, build a realistic prototype, test with five customers. See [[design-sprint]].
- A Decider makes the call, informed by silent voting, so the team avoids endless debate. See [[design-sprint]].
- Build a facade, not a product: a prototype just real enough to get honest reactions. See [[design-sprint]], [[four-product-risks]].

## Use it when

- A big risky decision with lots of opinions
- Testing a new concept fast
