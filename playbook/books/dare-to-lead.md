## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Courageous leadership requires vulnerability: willingness to show up when you can't control the outcome.
- Clear is kind; unclear is unkind. See [[care-personally-challenge-directly]].
- Live by a small number of named values, and build trust through specific behaviors.
