- **Author:** Kathy Sierra

## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- People don't want your product; they want to be awesome at what the product lets them do. See [[jobs-to-be-done]].
- Help users get skilled fast: reduce cognitive load and give them high-quality examples to learn from. See [[affordances-and-signifiers]].
- Sustained success comes from users' growing competence, not from persuasion tricks.
