# The Design of Everyday Things

- **Author:** Don Norman
- **Topics:** [[design-and-usability]]
- **On the list:** [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]]

## Why it's on the list

A design classic. After it you notice good and bad design everywhere. (see [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]])

## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Good design makes possible actions discoverable and understandable through affordances and signifiers. See [[affordances-and-signifiers]].
- Use natural mapping, clear feedback, constraints and a sound conceptual model. See [[affordances-and-signifiers]].
- Most "human error" is really design error. See [[affordances-and-signifiers]].
- Bridge the gulf of execution (how do I do it?) and the gulf of evaluation (did it work?). See [[affordances-and-signifiers]].

- Design reviews
