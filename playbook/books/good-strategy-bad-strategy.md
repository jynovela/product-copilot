# Good Strategy Bad Strategy

- **Author:** Richard Rumelt
- **Topics:** [[strategy-and-innovation]]

## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Bad strategy is fluff, failure to face the real challenge, mistaking goals for strategy, and vague objectives. See [[kernel-of-good-strategy]].
- The kernel of good strategy: a diagnosis of the challenge, a guiding policy to deal with it, and coherent actions that carry it out. See [[kernel-of-good-strategy]].
- Strategy draws power from leverage, focus, proximate objectives and coordinated action. See [[kernel-of-good-strategy]].

## Use it when

- Writing or reviewing a product strategy
- Spotting a strategy that is really a list of goals
