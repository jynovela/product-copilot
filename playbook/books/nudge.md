## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Every choice has a choice architecture: how options are presented changes what people pick. See [[choice-architecture]].
- Defaults are powerful; most people keep them. See [[choice-architecture]].
- Libertarian paternalism: steer toward better outcomes while keeping freedom to choose. See [[choice-architecture]].
- Good choice architecture gives feedback, expects error and simplifies complex choices. See [[affordances-and-signifiers]].
