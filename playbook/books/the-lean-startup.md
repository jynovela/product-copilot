## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Startups exist to learn what customers want; progress is validated learning. See [[build-measure-learn]].
- The build-measure-learn loop: ship a minimum viable product, measure, learn, repeat fast. See [[build-measure-learn]].
- Decide to pivot or persevere based on evidence. See [[build-measure-learn]].
- Use actionable metrics and innovation accounting, not vanity metrics. See [[statistical-traps]].
