## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

Confidence: lower. Some research associated with Ariely has faced replication problems, and one study he co-authored was retracted over fabricated data. Treat individual experiments with caution even where the broad ideas hold.

- We judge value by comparison; a decoy option can steer choices. See [[choice-architecture]].
- "Free" has outsized pull compared with a very low price. See [[choice-architecture]].
- Social norms and market norms clash: paying people can reduce goodwill effort.
- Anchors and ownership (we overvalue what we have) shape what people will pay. See [[system-1-and-system-2]].
