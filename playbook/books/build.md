# Build: An Unorthodox Guide to Making Things Worth Making

- **Author:** Tony Fadell
- **Topics:** [[strategy-and-innovation]], [[communication-and-storytelling]], [[leadership-and-management]]
- **On the list:** [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]]

## Why it's on the list

- Lessons from the creator of the iPod and founder of Nest, including the failures along the way. Ken calls it one of the best books by a product-minded entrepreneur. (see [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]])

## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Start with the story: be able to explain why the product should exist and what problem it solves before building it.
- Build painkillers, not vitamins: products that remove a real, felt pain win over nice-to-haves. See [[jobs-to-be-done]].
- Early versions are driven mostly by opinion and vision because there is little data yet; later versions should lean more on data. See [[resulting]].
- Design the whole customer journey, not just the product: discovery, purchase, unboxing, setup, support.
- Fight habituation: notice the everyday annoyances people have stopped seeing; those are product opportunities.

## Use it when

- Shaping a v1 when there is no data yet
- Making a product pitch or launch story
- Deciding what to cut from scope
