## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Write down founding documents (mission, principles, long-term goals) so people can act without asking.
- Run an operating system: a regular cadence for goals, planning, reviews and metrics. See [[okrs]].
- Know yourself as a manager and make your working style explicit to your team. See [[keystone-conversation]].
- Treat hiring, feedback and team development as core, repeatable management tactics. See [[one-on-ones]].
