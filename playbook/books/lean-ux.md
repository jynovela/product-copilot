## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Replace heavy deliverables with shared understanding and fast experiments. See [[build-measure-learn]].
- Frame work as hypotheses ("we believe... we'll know we're right when...") and test them with MVPs. See [[build-measure-learn]].
- Measure success by outcomes (changes in user behavior), not output. See [[build-trap]].
