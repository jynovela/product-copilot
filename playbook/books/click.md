## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

Confidence: lower. This book is recent (2025) and I know its details less well than older books. Treat specifics as approximate.

- Run a short Foundation Sprint before building: the team agrees on target customer, problem, competitors and its advantage. See [[foundation-sprint]].
- Differentiate deliberately: pick the few dimensions where you will be clearly different from the alternatives. See [[foundation-sprint]], [[positioning-in-the-mind]].
- Compare several approaches before committing to one, then write a founding hypothesis to test. See [[foundation-sprint]], [[design-sprint]].
