## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- People often fail to change because of hidden competing commitments, not lack of will. See [[immunity-map]].
- Map it in four columns: improvement goal, what you do instead, hidden competing commitments, and the big assumptions behind them. See [[immunity-map]].
- Test big assumptions with small, safe experiments rather than forcing behavior change. See [[immunity-map]].
- Adults can grow through stages of mind: socialized, self-authoring, self-transforming. See [[reactive-vs-creative-leadership]].
