## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- The PM's core skills are Communication, Organization, Research and Execution (CORE).
- Most PM work is connective: helping people understand each other and the customer.
- Be honest about the messy reality of the job instead of chasing an ideal process.
