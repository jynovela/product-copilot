# The Mythical Man-Month

- **Author:** Frederick P. Brooks Jr.
- **Topics:** [[product-teams-and-operating-model]]
- **On the list:** [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]], marked as a top pick

## Why it's on the list

Ken's one computer science pick. Nearly fifty years old and still relevant about mistakes engineering leaders keep making. (see [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]])

## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Brooks's law: adding people to a late software project makes it later. See [[brooks-law]].
- Conceptual integrity matters most; a few minds should shape the design.
- The second-system effect: a team's second system tends to be over-designed.
- Plan to throw one away; and later, there is no silver bullet for software productivity.

## Use it when

- A late project and pressure to add people
- Estimating and planning
