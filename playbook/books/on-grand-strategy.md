## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Grand strategy is the alignment of potentially unlimited aspirations with necessarily limited capabilities.
- Combine the fox (many small ideas, adaptability) with the hedgehog (one big idea, direction).
- Common sense and proportion matter more than rigid plans.

## Use it when

- Writing a product vision and strategy
- Matching ambition to team capacity
