# The Science of Storytelling

- **Author:** Will Storr
- **Topics:** [[communication-and-storytelling]]
- **On the list:** [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]]

## Why it's on the list

- Ken is often asked how to become a better storyteller. A novelist explains how the brain responds to stories and how to connect with an audience. (see [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]])

## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- The brain builds a model of the world and pays attention to change and surprise.
- Compelling stories center on a flawed character whose beliefs are challenged and who changes.
- Specific, concrete detail and a question the audience wants answered hold attention. See [[sticky-ideas-success]].
