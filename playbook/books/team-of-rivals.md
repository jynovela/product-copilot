# Team of Rivals

- **Author:** Doris Kearns Goodwin
- **Topics:** [[leadership-and-management]], [[negotiation-feedback-and-relationships]]
- **On the list:** [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]]

## Why it's on the list

Lincoln brought his rivals into his cabinet and turned them into advisors. A lesson in using others' wisdom, with or without formal authority. (see [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]])

## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Lincoln appointed his rivals for the nomination (Seward, Chase, Bates) to his cabinet.
- He won them over through emotional intelligence: magnanimity, sharing credit, taking blame, and holding firm on what mattered.
- Surrounding yourself with strong, dissenting voices improves decisions if you can hold the group together. See [[resulting]].

- Leading without authority
- Bringing critics into a decision
