## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

Confidence: lower. I know this book only in broad strokes.

- Designers should make useful, beautiful everyday things for everyone, not art for a few.
- Good design comes from understanding the object's function, materials and use.
- Simplicity is hard: removing what is unnecessary takes more work than adding.
