## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Term sheets mostly matter for two things: economics and control.
- Economic terms include valuation, liquidation preference, option pool and anti-dilution.
- Control terms include board seats, protective provisions and voting rights.
