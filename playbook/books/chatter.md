# Chatter: The Voice in Our Head, Why It Matters, and How to Harness It

- **Author:** Ethan Kross
- **Topics:** [[self-leadership-and-productivity]]
- **On the list:** [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]]

## Why it's on the list

Many product leaders fight a harsh inner critic that feeds imposter feelings. A psychologist explains the science of the inner voice and how to manage it. (see [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]])

## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- Negative, looping self-talk ("chatter") hurts focus, decisions, health and relationships.
- Create distance: talk to yourself by name or in the second person, imagine advising a friend, or picture how you'll see it in a year. See [[distanced-self-talk]].
- Normalize the experience: remind yourself that this is a common situation, not a personal failing. See [[distanced-self-talk]].
- When you vent to others, aim for help reframing, not just reliving the problem (co-rumination makes chatter worse).

## Use it when

- Before a high-stakes review or launch
- After a failed bet or harsh feedback
