# Yes to the Mess: Surprising Leadership Lessons from Jazz

- **Author:** Frank J. Barrett
- **Topics:** [[leadership-and-management]]
- **On the list:** [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]]

## Why it's on the list

Jazz is messy and risky every night. Barrett's lessons for leaders inspired Ken's essay on jazz and product management. (see [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]])

## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

Confidence: lower. I know the main themes better than the book's examples.

- Say yes to the mess: treat uncertainty and errors as material to build on.
- Minimal structure, maximum autonomy: just enough shared structure for people to improvise. See [[empowered-product-teams]].
- Take turns soloing and supporting; good followership is a leadership skill.
- Leaders can provoke growth by putting people in slightly uncomfortable situations.

- Designing team processes
