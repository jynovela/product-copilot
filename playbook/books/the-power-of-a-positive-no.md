# The Power of a Positive No

- **Author:** William Ury
- **Topics:** [[negotiation-feedback-and-relationships]]
- **On the list:** [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]]

## Why it's on the list

- Saying no gracefully and firmly is one of a PM's most important skills, and this is the classic guide. (see [[2026-01-07-ken-norton-best-pm-books]])

## Key ideas

General knowledge: the points below come from model memory, not from an ingested copy of the book. Check details before quoting or relying on them.

- A positive no is Yes! No. Yes?: say yes to your own interests, deliver a clear no, then propose a positive alternative. See [[positive-no]].
- Ground the no in what you are protecting, not in rejection of the other person. See [[positive-no]].
- Have a Plan B so you can say no without depending on their agreement. See [[positive-no]], [[principled-negotiation]].

## Use it when

- Declining feature requests
- Protecting the roadmap
