# System 1 and System 2

## What it is

General knowledge: System 1 is fast and automatic; System 2 is slow and effortful. System 1 drives most judgments and brings biases: anchoring, availability, representativeness, loss aversion, framing effects and overconfidence.

## How to use it

- Slow down for important, irreversible calls.
- Watch for anchors in estimates and pricing.
- Use the outside view: how long did similar projects take?

## Watch out for

- Believing you're immune once you know the biases.
