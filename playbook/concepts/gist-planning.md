# GIST planning

## What it is

General knowledge: Itamar Gilad's planning model. Goals are outcomes to reach. Ideas are possible ways to reach them, ranked by evidence (he uses ICE with a confidence meter). Step-projects are short projects that test or build an idea in steps. Tasks are the team's daily work. Plans change as evidence arrives.

## How to use it

- Keep goals stable and ideas fluid.
- Raise confidence in an idea with cheap tests before building it fully.

## Watch out for

- Turning step-projects back into a fixed feature roadmap.
