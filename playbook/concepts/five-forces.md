# Five forces and competitive advantage

## What it is

General knowledge: Porter's five forces explain industry profitability: rivalry among competitors, bargaining power of buyers and of suppliers, threat of substitutes, and threat of new entrants. Advantage comes from a distinct position built on trade-offs and fit between activities, not just doing the same things better.

## How to use it

- Map the five forces for your market.
- Name the trade-offs your product makes that rivals won't copy.

## Watch out for

- Confusing operational efficiency with strategy.
