# Choice architecture

## What it is

General knowledge: Every decision happens in a context someone designed. Defaults, the number and order of options, decoys, framing and the price of zero all change what people choose.

## How to use it

- Pick defaults deliberately, in the user's interest.
- Limit options at key decisions.
- Test how framing and comparison change choices.

## Watch out for

- Defaults chosen for the company at the user's expense.
