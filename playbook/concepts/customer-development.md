# Customer development

## What it is

General knowledge: Steve Blank's process: startups search for a repeatable business model by getting out of the building. Customer discovery and customer validation test hypotheses; customer creation and company building scale what works.

## How to use it

- Write your business-model hypotheses down.
- Talk to customers before writing code.

## Watch out for

- Scaling sales and marketing before validation.
