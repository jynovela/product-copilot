# WRAP decision process

## What it is

General knowledge: The Heath brothers' process against four decision villains (narrow framing, confirmation bias, short-term emotion, overconfidence): widen your options, reality-test your assumptions, attain distance before deciding, prepare to be wrong.

## How to use it

- Turn either/or choices into at least three options.
- Run small tests ("ooch") before big commitments.
- Set tripwires that trigger a rethink.

## Watch out for

- Only looking for evidence that supports the favored option.
