# Ansoff matrix

## What it is

General knowledge: Igor Ansoff's 1957 product-market grid. Market penetration (existing product, existing market), product development (new product, existing market), market development (existing product, new market) and diversification (both new, highest risk).

## How to use it

- Label each roadmap bet with its quadrant to see how much risk you are taking.
- Expect diversification to need the most evidence.

## Watch out for

- Treating diversification as low risk because the team is confident.

## What the framework articles say

## What the research digests say

- Hanover Research and Atlassian list market penetration, market development, product development and diversification as product strategy types; Atlassian adds cost competition and quality differentiation. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-strategy]])
- Inference from the digest: these are growth directions, not strategies; a strategy still needs choices about where to play and how to win.
