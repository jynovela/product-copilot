# Value Proposition Canvas

## What it is

General knowledge: Osterwalder's canvas has two halves: the customer profile (jobs to be done, pains, gains) and the value map (products and services, pain relievers, gain creators). Fit is when the value map addresses the most important jobs, pains and gains.

## How to use it

- Fill the customer side from research, not assumptions.
- Rank pains and gains by importance.

## Watch out for

- Filling the canvas in a workshop without customer evidence.
