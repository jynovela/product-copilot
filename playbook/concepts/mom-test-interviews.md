# Mom Test interviews

## What it is

General knowledge: Customer conversations go wrong when you pitch and ask for opinions. Instead talk about their life, ask about specific past events, listen more than you talk, ignore compliments, and look for commitments (time, money, reputation).

## How to use it

- Ask "tell me about the last time..." questions.
- End by asking for a concrete next step to test commitment.

## Watch out for

- "Would you use this?" questions.
- Counting compliments as validation.
