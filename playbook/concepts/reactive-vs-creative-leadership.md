# Reactive versus creative leadership

## What it is

General knowledge: Developmental models describe a shift from reactive leadership (driven by approval, control, protection; "below the line", committed to being right) to creative leadership (driven by purpose and vision; "above the line", committed to learning).

## How to use it

- Notice signs of being below the line: blame, defensiveness, needing to be right.
- Shift by asking what you can learn and what you are responsible for.

## Watch out for

- Using the labels to judge others instead of noticing yourself.
