# Principles of persuasion

## What it is

General knowledge: Cialdini's principles describe mental shortcuts that make people comply: reciprocity, commitment and consistency, social proof, liking, authority, scarcity, and (added later) unity.

## How to use it

- Use honest social proof (real usage, real reviews) at decision points.
- Ask for small commitments before big ones.
- Audit flows for fake scarcity or pressure.

## Watch out for

- Dark patterns that win a conversion and lose trust.
