# Technology adoption lifecycle and the chasm

## What it is

General knowledge: Customers adopt new technology in groups: innovators, early adopters, early majority, late majority, laggards. Early adopters want breakthroughs; the early majority want proven, complete solutions and references from peers. The gap between them is the chasm.

## How to use it

- Pick one narrow beachhead segment of pragmatists.
- Deliver the whole product they need, including services and integrations.
- Win references in that segment before expanding.

## Watch out for

- Mistaking early adopter enthusiasm for mainstream demand.
