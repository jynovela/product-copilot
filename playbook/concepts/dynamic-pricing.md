# Dynamic pricing

## What it is

Change the price in real time with demand, time, stock, competitors or the buyer, as in airline and hotel yield management and Uber's surge pricing (Revology, Dovetail, Unleashed). Related ideas: personalized pricing (per buyer) and market pricing such as fuel, which follows supply and demand. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-pricing]])

## How to use it

- Be transparent about how prices move; it builds trust (Esade, citing Marco Bertini). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-pricing]])
- Budget for the data and technology it needs; that makes it costly for small firms (Unleashed). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-pricing]])

## Watch out for

- Buyer backlash when they learn others paid less (Unleashed); Wendy's is the example given (Product School). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-pricing]])
- Price discrimination on protected traits is illegal (PMA). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-pricing]])
- Paddle (a vendor) says it doesn't suit SaaS subscriptions. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-pricing]])
