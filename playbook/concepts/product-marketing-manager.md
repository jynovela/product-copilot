# Product marketing manager (PMM)

## What it is

General knowledge: A PMM decides how a product is positioned, who it is for, how it is described, and how it reaches the market: launches, sales enablement, pricing input and competitive intelligence. PMMs often sit in marketing. The PM owns what gets built and why; the PMM owns how it is taken to market. The two should share customer research and work together from discovery, not only at launch.

## How to use it

- Bring the PMM into discovery so positioning shapes the product, not just the launch.
- Agree who owns pricing, competitive analysis and customer research; these overlap most.

## Watch out for

- Treating the PMM as the person who writes launch emails.
- PMs and PMMs running separate customer interviews and reaching different conclusions.

## What the articles say

These are vendor, training-company, recruiter and practitioner articles, not neutral sources.

- Owns positioning, messaging, pricing and go-to-market to drive adoption. (see [[2026-11-21-juan-types-of-pms-summary]])
- Bridges product and marketing; positions, launches and drives demand; is the voice of the customer and runs research; less involved in how the product is built. (see [[2024-04-03-product-school-18-types-of-pms]])
- Often sits within marketing rather than product; owns positioning, messaging, go-to-market and demand generation. (see [[2026-06-25-recruiting-from-scratch-pm-job-titles]])
- Positions the product and drives adoption through go-to-market with sales and marketing. (see [[agilemania-types-of-product-managers]])
- A marketing manager first and a product manager second. (see [[product-faculty-types-of-product-managers]])
- Aligns positioning with market needs to drive adoption and revenue; listed as a PM type. (see [[2026-02-22-creately-15-types-of-pms]])
- Owns positioning, messaging and go-to-market for existing products and feeds customer feedback back to PMs. (see [[2023-01-23-ally-mexicotte-types-of-pms]])
- The consumer whisperer who understands users and marketing and helps set price and positioning. (see [[2022-08-31-tiwatayo-kunle-types-of-pms]])
- Calls it marketing product manager or PMM; externally focused on messaging and revenue strategy. (see [[2025-03-05-specialist-pm-jobs-overview]])
- An adjacent role, not a type of PM. (see [[2025-03-25-prodpad-12-types-of-pms]])
- Mitchell's marketing PM archetype suits people with advertising, PR or marketing backgrounds. (see [[stanford-online-types-of-pm-roles]])

## Contradictions

- Product School, Agilemania and Creately list PMM as a type of PM (see [[2024-04-03-product-school-18-types-of-pms]], [[agilemania-types-of-product-managers]], [[2026-02-22-creately-15-types-of-pms]]). ProdPad says it is adjacent, and Recruiting from Scratch says it often sits in marketing (see [[2025-03-25-prodpad-12-types-of-pms]], [[2026-06-25-recruiting-from-scratch-pm-job-titles]]). The coaching platform treats marketing PM and PMM as the same role (see [[2025-03-05-specialist-pm-jobs-overview]]), while Mitchell's marketing PM (via Stanford Online) is a PM with a marketing background (see [[stanford-online-types-of-pm-roles]]).
