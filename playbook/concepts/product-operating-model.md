# Product operating model

## What it is

General knowledge: A set of changes in how a company builds (small, frequent, reliable releases), solves problems (empowered cross-functional teams doing discovery) and decides which problems to solve (product vision and strategy).

## How to use it

- Assess the company on each of the three dimensions.
- Start with one or a few teams and show results.
- Get executive sponsorship; most of the change is cultural.

## Watch out for

- Renaming roles without changing how decisions are made.
