# CIRCLES method

## What it is

General knowledge: Lewis C. Lin's framework: Comprehend the situation, Identify the customer, Report the customer's needs, Cut through prioritization, List solutions, Evaluate trade-offs, Summarize your recommendation.

## How to use it

- Use it to practice product design questions out loud.
- Spend most time on customer and needs, not solutions.

## Watch out for

- Reciting the acronym instead of thinking.

## What the framework articles say

These are vendor and practitioner articles, not neutral sources.
