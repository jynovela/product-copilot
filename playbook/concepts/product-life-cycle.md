# Product life cycle

## What it is

General knowledge: A marketing model of a product's sales over time: introduction (slow, educating the market), growth (fast, competitors arrive), maturity (peak, defending share) and decline. It's a rough description, not a law; products can be revived or skip stages.

## How to use it

- Name the stage your product is in and check that goals and metrics match it.
- In maturity, look for the next S-curve before decline.

## Watch out for

- Assuming decline is inevitable and under-investing too early.
