# Keystone conversation

## What it is

General knowledge: At the start of a working relationship, talk explicitly about how each of you works at your best, past good and bad experiences, preferences, and how you'll handle it when things go wrong. Revisit it over time.

## How to use it

- Have it in the first week with a new engineering lead, designer or manager.
- Agree on how you'll raise problems.

## Watch out for

- Waiting for a conflict before discussing how you work.
