# Positive no

## What it is

General knowledge: A way to say no while keeping the relationship: start from the yes you're protecting (your interests), give a clear no, then propose a positive alternative.

## How to use it

- Name what the no protects (focus, quality, the strategy).
- Offer what you can do instead.

## Watch out for

- A vague maybe that the other side hears as yes.
