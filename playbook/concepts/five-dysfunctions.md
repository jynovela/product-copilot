# Five dysfunctions of a team

## What it is

General knowledge: Lencioni's pyramid: without vulnerability-based trust, teams avoid conflict; without conflict, they don't commit; without commitment, they avoid holding each other accountable; then they stop focusing on collective results.

## How to use it

- Diagnose which level your team is stuck at.
- Leaders go first in showing vulnerability.

## Watch out for

- Mistaking artificial harmony for trust.
