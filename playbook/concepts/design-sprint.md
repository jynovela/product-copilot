# Design sprint

## What it is

General knowledge: Over five days a small team maps the problem and picks a target, sketches solutions individually, decides with structured voting and a Decider, builds a realistic prototype, and tests it with five target customers.

## How to use it

- Use for a big, risky question where the team has many opinions.
- Name a Decider up front.
- Recruit five target customers before the week starts.

## Watch out for

- Running sprints for small or already-decided questions.
