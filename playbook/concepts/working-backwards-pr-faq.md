# Working backwards (PR/FAQ)

## What it is

General knowledge: Amazon's practice: before building, write a one-page press release announcing the finished product to customers, plus an FAQ for customers and internal stakeholders. Teams debate the document, not slides.

## How to use it

- Write the PR/FAQ before committing a team.
- If the press release isn't exciting, rethink the product.

## Watch out for

- Writing it after the decision is made.

## What the framework articles say

These are vendor and practitioner articles, not neutral sources.

- Amazon's method: write a press release for the end user describing the problem, why other options fall short and how the product succeeds. (see [[productplan-37-pm-frameworks]])
- Starts by imagining the product is built; the press release describes the problem and the value above alternatives. (see [[neal-cabage-21-pm-frameworks]])
- Starts with a customer-facing press release and FAQ to clarify value before building. (see [[2026-11-20-juan-pm-frameworks-list]])
