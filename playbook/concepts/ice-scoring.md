# ICE scoring

## What it is

General knowledge: Popularized by Sean Ellis for growth teams. Each idea gets a score for impact, confidence and ease, often 1 to 10, and the scores are averaged or multiplied. Fast, but less rigorous than RICE because it has no reach and the scales are loose.

## How to use it

- Use it to sort a long list of small experiments.
- Keep one person or a small group scoring for consistency.

## Watch out for

- Scores drift between people.
- Using it for big strategic bets.
