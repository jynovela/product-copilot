# Habit design

## What it is

General knowledge: Habits form from cue, craving, response and reward. Clear's four laws invert to break bad habits. Small, repeated behaviors compound.

## How to use it

- For personal habits, attach new ones to existing routines.
- For products, make the core action easy and rewarding, and give a clear cue.

## Watch out for

- Engagement tricks that serve metrics more than users.
