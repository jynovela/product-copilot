# SPADE

## What it is

Gokul Rajaram's method from Square, for hard decisions that must be made quickly. Setting: what exactly is being decided, by when, and why now. People: a Responsible person who decides and is accountable, an Approver who vetoes the quality of the process (not the decision), and Consultants who give input. Alternatives: feasible, diverse, comprehensive options beyond the obvious binary. Decide: consultants vote privately first to avoid groupthink, then the responsible person decides. Explain: communicate the decision and its reasons. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-decisions]])

## How to use it

- Add the value metric affected to the Setting (Abraham). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-decisions]])
- Collect private votes before discussion. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-decisions]])
- Rajaram says most companies rely on consensus for hard calls and that it doesn't work (Zeda). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-decisions]])

## Watch out for

- "Approver" and "Responsible" mean different things here than in DACI and RACI. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-decisions]])
