# Loonshots and franchises

## What it is

General knowledge: Bahcall's model: loonshots (dismissed early ideas) and franchises (proven lines) need different teams, incentives and cultures, with a deliberate handoff between them.

## How to use it

- Separate exploration teams from core product teams.
- Design the handoff from exploration to scale.

## Watch out for

- The leader becoming the sole judge of new ideas (the Moses trap).
