# 7 Powers

## What it is

Seven hard-to-copy advantages, from Hamilton Helmer's book *7 Powers* (2016), as described by Hustle Badger: network effects; scale economies; switching costs; counter-positioning (a model incumbents can't copy without damaging their own business); cornered resource; branding; and process power. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-strategy]])

## How to use it

- Decide powers at product level, not squad level (Hustle Badger). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-strategy]])
- Hustle Badger's heuristic: if your solution doesn't generate 2 or 3 powers, expect trouble keeping margins. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-strategy]])
- Check a power's strength by finding companies that built durable businesses on similar advantages, and by asking independent investors or experts. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-strategy]])
- Technical defensibility is a related list: data network effects, integration depth, API ecosystems (Phenomenon Studio). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-strategy]])

## Watch out for

- Only secondhand in the research; read the book before relying on the details. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-strategy]])
