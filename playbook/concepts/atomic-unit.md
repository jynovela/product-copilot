# Atomic unit

## What it is

General knowledge: Fred Wilson's idea: identify the core unit of value in your product and make it excellent, linkable and shareable.

## How to use it

- Name your product's atomic unit.
- Check that it can be shared and linked on its own.

## Watch out for

- Polishing features around a weak core unit.
