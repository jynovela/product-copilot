# Braintrust

## What it is

General knowledge: A group of experienced peers reviews work in progress and gives frank notes. The owner decides what to do with them. Candor works because the group has no authority to force changes.

## How to use it

- Run regular candid reviews of early product work.
- Keep authority with the team that owns the work.

## Watch out for

- Reviews that become approval gates.
