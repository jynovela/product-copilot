# Resulting

## What it is

General knowledge: Outcomes mix decision quality and luck. Resulting means calling a decision good because it turned out well, or bad because it didn't, which teaches the wrong lessons.

## How to use it

- In launch reviews, separate what you knew and decided from what luck did.
- Write down beliefs and confidence before deciding.
- Ask "wanna bet?" to test how sure people are.

## Watch out for

- Changing a sound process after one bad outcome.
