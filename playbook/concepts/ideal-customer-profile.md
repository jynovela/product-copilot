# Ideal customer profile

## What it is

An ideal customer profile (ICP) is "a high-fit subset of your broader market" (PMA), described at company level (industry, size, region, tech stack); personas describe individuals (Product School). AEVO frames ICPs as segments that feel the pain most, have budget and urgency, and benefit most. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-go-to-market]])

## How to use it

- Describe it in objective, checkable terms; "ambitious" is not checkable (Aventi). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-go-to-market]])
- Pick a narrow target first and expand later; Oatly's US entry started with baristas in four cities before grocery shoppers (PMA). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-go-to-market]])
- Update it from usage and CRM data (Product School's "living segmentation"). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-go-to-market]])
- Build personas from existing customers in the CRM, interview them, and review quarterly (Salesforce). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-go-to-market]])

## Watch out for

- The wrong target customer (too small, unwilling to pay, hard to reach) is the most common root cause of GTM failure (Dovetail). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-go-to-market]])
