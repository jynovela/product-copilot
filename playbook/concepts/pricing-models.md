# Pricing models

## What it is

The pricing model is the format the customer pays in; the pricing strategy sets the level (Qualtrics, Dovetail: strategy is internal, model is shown to customers). In SaaS the model is often called packaging. Aha! lists six SaaS models: flat rate, per user, usage-based, tiered, feature-based and freemium. Others add subscription, transaction-based, hybrid and outcome-based. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-pricing]])

## How to use it

- Flat-rate subscriptions are simple and predictable but one size fits all, and cost to serve varies by customer (Qualtrics). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-pricing]])
- Per user fits when all users need the same features (LogRocket). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-pricing]])
- For AI features, ask ProdPad's three questions: what does it cost to serve, how predictable must the buyer's bill be, and can the outcome be measured? (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-pricing]])
- Market data: Kyle Poyar's 2026 poll of 230 companies (sponsored by a billing vendor) found hybrid pricing at 37%, up from 25%, AI credits at 29%, and 3 in 4 companies changed pricing in the last year. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-pricing]])

## Watch out for

- Several sources mix strategies and models in one list (LogRocket, Aha!); keep them apart. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-pricing]])
- Product School adds a third layer, the monetization model: how the business makes money at all. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-pricing]])

## Contradictions

- ProdPad treats hybrid as where most companies land; Poyar's poll puts it at 37%. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-pricing]])
