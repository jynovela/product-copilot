# Impact mapping

## What it is

General knowledge: Gojko Adzic's technique: a mind map from a business goal (why), to actors (who), to the impact on their behavior (how), to deliverables (what). It keeps features tied to a behavior change that serves a goal.

## How to use it

- Start with one measurable goal.
- Describe impacts as behavior changes, not features.
- Cut deliverables that don't connect to an impact.

## Watch out for

- Mapping to justify features already chosen.
