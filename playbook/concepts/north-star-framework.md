# North Star framework

## What it is

General knowledge: Pick one North Star metric that reflects customer value and predicts long-term business results, such as nights booked or weekly active teams. Break it into a few input metrics that teams can influence directly. It aligns teams on value, not output.

## How to use it

- Choose a metric that rises only when customers get value.
- Map three to five input metrics and give teams ownership of them.
- Pair it with guardrail metrics so teams don't game it.

## Watch out for

- Picking revenue, which lags and isn't customer value.
- Vanity metrics like sign-ups.

## What the framework articles say

These are vendor and practitioner articles, not neutral sources.

- Introduced by Amplitude; quotes Sean Ellis that the metric best captures the core value delivered; complemented by key inputs. (see [[neal-cabage-21-pm-frameworks]])
- The single core metric that best captures the value the product delivers. (see [[2026-11-20-juan-pm-frameworks-list]])

## What the research digests say

- Gupta's criteria: it measures customer value, reflects strategy, leads revenue (if it rises while revenue is flat, monetization is broken) and isn't a vanity metric. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-metrics]])
- Stress test: ask stakeholders "If we could only improve one number for the next 12 months…" and see whether they agree (Gupta). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-metrics]])
- Examples by model (Gupta): weekly active teams completing the core action for SaaS, successful transactions for marketplaces, weekly repeat purchasers for e-commerce, time spent for media. All examples in the sources are unsourced. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-metrics]])
- Feature success metrics roll up to team OKRs, which roll up to the North Star (Siegel). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-metrics]])
- A single number invites gaming and tunnel vision; pair it with counter-metrics. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-metrics]])

## Contradictions

- Neal Cabage says Amplitude introduced the framework while quoting Sean Ellis's definition (see [[neal-cabage-21-pm-frameworks]]). General knowledge: Sean Ellis popularized the North Star metric in growth circles, and Amplitude later published a North Star Framework playbook. Amplitude is an analytics vendor, so its version is also a pitch for its product.
