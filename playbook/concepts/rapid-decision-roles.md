# RAPID decision roles

## What it is

Bain's five roles for each major decision: Recommend (builds the proposal with data, options, risks and a recommended direction), Agree (must sign off, usually legal, finance or regulatory; assign only when required), Perform (carries it out; brought in early as a reality check), Input (advises, no authority), Decide (one person makes the final call and owns the result). Bain owns RAPID as a trademark. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-decisions]])

## How to use it

- One Decider, "never two", and never "the committee"; if more than two people must Agree, "you're basically creating a small senate" (Wing). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-decisions]])
- Write the roles down in a short doc, and check afterwards whether the Decider became a bottleneck (Wing). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-decisions]])
- Use it for high-impact decisions that touch money, customers or risk (Wing); don't assign roles unless the decision's value justifies it (Bain). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-decisions]])
- Bain's What, Who, How, When: frame the decision well, assign roles, agree criteria and present more than one real alternative, then set deadlines so the hurdle to reopen is high. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-decisions]])

## Watch out for

- Heavy for small tasks (Wing, Bain). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-decisions]])
- RAPID's Agree is a required sign-off, not the decision; DACI's Approver is the decision. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-decisions]])
