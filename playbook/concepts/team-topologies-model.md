# Team Topologies model

## What it is

General knowledge: Skelton and Pais: stream-aligned teams own a flow of change for users; platform teams provide internal services; enabling teams coach; complicated-subsystem teams own specialist parts. Teams interact by collaboration, X-as-a-service or facilitating. Limit each team's cognitive load, and use Conway's law (systems mirror communication structures) deliberately.

## How to use it

- As a platform PM, treat internal teams as customers and offer the platform as a service.
- Check whether team boundaries match the architecture you want.

## Watch out for

- Platform teams that become ticket queues.
