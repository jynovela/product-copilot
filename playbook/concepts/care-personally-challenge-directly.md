# Radical candor

## What it is

General knowledge: Kim Scott's framework: feedback works when you both care personally and challenge directly. Failing one or both gives obnoxious aggression, ruinous empathy or manipulative insincerity.

## How to use it

- Ask for feedback before giving it.
- Give small, specific feedback often, in person.

## Watch out for

- Ruinous empathy: staying quiet to be nice.
