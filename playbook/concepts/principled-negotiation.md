# Principled negotiation and BATNA

## What it is

General knowledge: From Getting to Yes: separate people from the problem, focus on interests not positions, invent options for mutual gain, insist on objective criteria. Your BATNA (best alternative to a negotiated agreement) sets when to walk away.

## How to use it

- Before a roadmap negotiation, write both sides' interests and your BATNA.
- Offer several options, not one take-it-or-leave-it plan.

## Watch out for

- Arguing over positions ("we need X by Q2") instead of interests.
