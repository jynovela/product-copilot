# Buy a feature

## What it is

General knowledge: One of Luke Hohmann's Innovation Games. Price each feature by its cost, give a small group a budget that can't buy everything, and watch what they buy and how they negotiate. Price some items above any one person's budget so people must pool money.

## How to use it

- Run with up to about eight customers from one segment.
- Listen to the negotiation; the reasons matter more than the totals.

## Watch out for

- It only ranks features you already listed.
- Hard to get customers together at the same time.

## What the framework articles say

These are vendor and practitioner articles, not neutral sources.

- Up to eight participants; price some features beyond one budget to force pooling; only evaluates features already on the roadmap. (see [[tempo-9-prioritization-frameworks]])
- Participants get a hypothetical sum and buy the features they most want built. (see [[productplan-37-pm-frameworks]])
