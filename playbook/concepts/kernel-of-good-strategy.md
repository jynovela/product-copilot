# Kernel of good strategy

## What it is

General knowledge: Rumelt: a good strategy has a kernel of three parts: a diagnosis that names the critical challenge, a guiding policy for dealing with it, and coherent actions that carry it out. Bad strategy is fluff, avoids the challenge, or mistakes goals for strategy.

## How to use it

- Write your product strategy as diagnosis, guiding policy and actions.
- Check every roadmap theme traces back to the diagnosis.

## Watch out for

- A list of goals or values presented as strategy.
