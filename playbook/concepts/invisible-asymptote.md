# Invisible asymptote

## What it is

General knowledge: Eugene Wei's idea: every product has a growth ceiling set by something customers dislike or can't do, often visible early if you ask.

## How to use it

- Ask customers and churned users what stops them using it more.
- Look for the ceiling before growth flattens.

## Watch out for

- Explaining slowing growth only by market size.
