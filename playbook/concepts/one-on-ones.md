# One-on-ones

## What it is

General knowledge: A recurring meeting where the report sets the agenda and the manager learns, coaches and unblocks. Grove argued it's one of a manager's highest-leverage activities.

## How to use it

- Hold them regularly; the report brings the agenda.
- Use them for coaching and problems, not status updates.

## Watch out for

- Cancelling them when busy.
- Turning them into status reports.
