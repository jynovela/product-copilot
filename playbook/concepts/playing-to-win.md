# Where to play and how to win

## What it is

Two of the strategy choices from *Playing to Win* (2013) by A.G. Lafley and Roger Martin, as described by Userpilot and Ant Murphy. Where to play: the segments, use cases, channels and geographies in scope, and which to exclude. How to win: the capabilities, data advantages or experience qualities competitors can't copy quickly. Martin: "Strategy needs to have a theory" (via Murphy). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-strategy]])

## How to use it

- Name what you exclude; a strategy that serves every segment with every feature "has deferred a bet" (Userpilot). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-strategy]])
- Murphy's four steps: gather inputs (don't spend months), build strategic narratives (insights plus a hypothesis about the future), find leverage (takes longest), then make choices that feel hard; if a choice doesn't eliminate work it is too high-level. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-strategy]])
- Strategy as "insights raised to the level of conviction" (John Cutler, via Userpilot). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-strategy]])

## Watch out for

- Userpilot credits the book to Martin alone; it is by Lafley and Martin. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-strategy]])
