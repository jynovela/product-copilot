# Sticky ideas (SUCCESs)

## What it is

General knowledge: Six traits make ideas memorable: Simple, Unexpected, Concrete, Credible, Emotional, Stories. The main obstacle is the curse of knowledge.

## How to use it

- Find the core message in one sentence.
- Replace abstractions with a concrete customer example.
- Check your doc against the six traits.

## Watch out for

- Expert jargon caused by the curse of knowledge.
