# Mimetic desire

## What it is

General knowledge: From René Girard: most desires are borrowed from models we imitate. Distant models inspire; close models create rivalry.

## How to use it

- Ask whose behavior your customers imitate.
- Make adoption visible among peers.

## Watch out for

- Copying competitors' roadmaps because they are your models.
