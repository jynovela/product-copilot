# DACI and RACI

## What it is

General knowledge: DACI names a Driver (runs the decision), an Approver (makes it, ideally one person), Contributors (give input) and the Informed (told after). RACI names who is Responsible for doing the work, who is Accountable, who is Consulted and who is Informed. DACI fits decisions; RACI fits ongoing work.

## How to use it

- Write the DACI at the top of any decision document.
- Keep one approver.
- Tell the informed group when the decision is made and why.

## Watch out for

- Several approvers, so no one decides.
- Filling it in once and ignoring it.

## What the framework articles say

These are vendor and practitioner articles, not neutral sources.

- Driver, approver (who makes the decision), contributors, and the informed (whose work could be affected). (see [[productplan-37-pm-frameworks]])
- Driver, approver, contributor, informed; similar to RACI but suited to PM because it stresses ownership rather than delivery. (see [[neal-cabage-21-pm-frameworks]])
- Clarifies decision roles and responsibilities across cross-functional teams. (see [[2026-11-20-juan-pm-frameworks-list]])

## What the research digests say

- DACI: Driver gathers people and information, Approver is the one person who decides, Contributors have "a voice but no vote", Informed are told (Atlassian). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-decisions]])
- RACI names who does the work but no single decision-maker (Asana, Wing). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-decisions]])
- Wing: DACI suits product and cross-functional work but is "less structured" than [[rapid-decision-roles]]. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-decisions]])
- The same words mean different things across frameworks: DACI's Approver decides, SPADE's Approver only vetoes the process. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-decisions]])
