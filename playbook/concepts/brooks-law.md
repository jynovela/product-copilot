# Brooks's law

## What it is

General knowledge: New people need ramp-up time and add communication overhead, which grows quickly with team size, so adding people late slows a project down.

## How to use it

- Cut scope or move dates before adding people late.
- Keep teams small and interfaces clear.

## Watch out for

- Treating person-months as interchangeable.
