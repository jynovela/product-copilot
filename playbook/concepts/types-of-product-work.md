# Types of product work

## What it is

General knowledge: Before product-market fit, the only work that matters is finding fit (zero-to-one). After fit, Casey Winters and Fareed Mosavat describe four types: feature work (extend what the product does), growth work (capture more of the existing market), scaling work (keep the ability to ship as you grow), and PMF expansion (move into adjacent products or markets). Company stage changes the PM job in a similar way: startup PMs do everything, growth-stage PMs optimize, large-company PMs coordinate.

## How to use it

- Name the type of work before choosing metrics, process or the PM to staff it.
- Don't run growth experiments on a product that hasn't found fit.
- Expect a PM great at one type to struggle at another; plan support.

## Watch out for

- Judging a zero-to-one team by growth metrics.
- Staffing scaling work with someone who wants to explore.

## What the articles say

These are vendor, training-company, recruiter and practitioner articles, not neutral sources.

- Summarizes Winters and Mosavat's four types of work after fit (feature, growth, scaling, PMF expansion); before fit, nothing else matters. Different people suit each type. (see [[2024-03-05-adam-fishman-pm-archetypes]])
- A 0-to-1 PM launches a new product to find fit, with little data and a focus on vision, structure and go-to-market; titles include innovation PM and most startup PM roles. (see [[2023-01-23-ally-mexicotte-types-of-pms]])
- Early-product PMs research, prototype and validate business models; mature-product PMs polish for existing customers and ensure quality. (see [[online-school-types-of-product-manager]])
- PM types also follow company maturity: startup PMs are a Swiss Army knife, often with no PM mentor; enterprise PMs have less independence and a slower pace. (see [[2021-07-29-lilia-gorbachik-pm-types]])
- Startup PMs help build the company, may build a 0-to-1 product, talk to investors and work with very limited resources. (see [[2025-03-25-prodpad-12-types-of-pms]])
- The startup PM is a jack of all trades who works with the founders on strategy and roadmap. (see [[2022-08-31-tiwatayo-kunle-types-of-pms]])
- Startup PMs cover a broad range of PM work across the lifecycle. (see [[aha-different-types-of-product-managers]])
