# The three conversations

## What it is

General knowledge: Difficult conversations carry three layers: the what-happened conversation (facts, intent, blame), the feelings conversation, and the identity conversation (what this says about me).

## How to use it

- Separate intent from impact.
- Map contributions instead of blame.
- Start from the third story, the neutral observer's view.

## Watch out for

- Arguing facts while the real issue is feelings or identity.
