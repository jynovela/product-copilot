# Extreme ownership

## What it is

General knowledge: From Willink and Babin: leaders take responsibility for everything, check their ego, keep plans simple, prioritize and execute, and push decisions down through decentralized command.

## How to use it

- In a retro, start with what you could have done differently.
- Make sure every person understands the why behind the mission.

## Watch out for

- Taking ownership as a show while still blaming others.
