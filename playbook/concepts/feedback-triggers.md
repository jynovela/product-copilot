# Feedback triggers

## What it is

General knowledge: Feedback is appreciation, coaching or evaluation. We reject it when it seems wrong (truth trigger), when we react to the giver (relationship trigger) or when it threatens our self-image (identity trigger).

## How to use it

- Name which trigger you feel before responding.
- Ask what kind of feedback the other person means.

## Watch out for

- Switchtracking: answering the feedback with a counter-complaint.
