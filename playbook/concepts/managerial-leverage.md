# Managerial leverage

## What it is

General knowledge: A manager's output equals the output of their organization plus the organizations they influence. High-leverage activities affect many people, a long time, or a key decision.

## How to use it

- Audit your calendar for leverage.
- Delegate tasks where others can learn, and keep oversight.
- Intervene early where small inputs change big outcomes.

## Watch out for

- Doing IC work because it feels productive.
