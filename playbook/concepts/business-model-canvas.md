# Business Model Canvas

## What it is

General knowledge: Alex Osterwalder's canvas: customer segments, value propositions, channels, customer relationships, revenue streams, key resources, key activities, key partners and cost structure. It pairs with the Value Proposition Canvas, which zooms into segments and value propositions.

## How to use it

- Fill one canvas per segment or model option.
- Mark each block as a tested fact or a guess, and test the riskiest guesses.

## Watch out for

- Filling it once and filing it away.

## What the framework articles say

These are vendor and practitioner articles, not neutral sources.

- A high-level view of how the product team creates value; often used with the Value Proposition Canvas. (see [[neal-cabage-21-pm-frameworks]])
- Says the canvas has nine building blocks, then lists five. (see [[techreviewer-popular-pm-frameworks]])

## Contradictions

- Techreviewer says the canvas has nine blocks but lists only five, and describes customer relationships as "your customers" (see [[techreviewer-popular-pm-frameworks]]). General knowledge: the nine blocks are listed above; customer segments and customer relationships are separate.
