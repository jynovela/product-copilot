# Four disciplines of execution

## What it is

FranklinCovey's 4DX (a vendor method): focus on one Wildly Important Goal (WIG); act on lead measures, not only lag measures; keep a compelling scoreboard; and hold a cadence of accountability with weekly check-ins. Goal formula: "From X to Y by When". (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-goals]])

## How to use it

- Pick one WIG per team; airfocus lists 4DX among goal frameworks for product strategy (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-strategy]]).
- Choose lead measures the team can move this week that predict the lag measure. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-goals]])
- Keep the scoreboard visible and simple. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-goals]])

## Watch out for

- One goal at a time conflicts with the 3 to 5 objectives most OKR sources allow. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-goals]])
