# Golden Circle

## What it is

General knowledge: Simon Sinek's model: inspiring organizations and leaders start with why (purpose), then how (approach), then what (products).

## How to use it

- Open strategy and launch docs with the why.
- Check the product (what) is consistent with the stated why.

## Watch out for

- A why that is just a marketing line.
