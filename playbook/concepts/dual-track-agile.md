# Dual-track agile

## What it is

General knowledge: The same cross-functional team works on two tracks at once. Discovery quickly tests ideas for value, usability, feasibility and viability. Delivery builds the ideas that passed into production quality. Discovery feeds the delivery backlog with validated work.

## How to use it

- Have the product trio spend time on discovery every week.
- Only move ideas to delivery once their riskiest assumptions are tested.

## Watch out for

- Separate discovery and delivery teams that throw work over a wall.
- Discovery that turns into a design phase.

## What the framework articles say

These are vendor and practitioner articles, not neutral sources.

- A cross-functional team splits work into discovery, which generates validated ideas, and delivery, which turns them into market-ready products. (see [[productplan-37-pm-frameworks]])
