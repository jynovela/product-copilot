# Management by objectives

## What it is

From Peter Drucker's *The Practice of Management* (1954): managers review organization goals, then agree annual objectives with each employee, cascading down, with periodic reviews. OKRs are described as Andy Grove's refinement of MBO that added key results and made it more collaborative (Wrike, Atlassian). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-goals]])

## How to use it

- Use it as history: it explains why OKR sources warn against top-down cascades and annual-only cycles. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-goals]])

## Watch out for

- Top-down, static and bureaucratic (Adobe Workfront, Perdoo, Meegle, Atlassian). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-goals]])
- Strict cascades push teams toward outputs; Herbig prefers "weak links, not strong ties". (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-goals]])
