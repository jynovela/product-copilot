# Weighted scoring

## What it is

General knowledge: Choose criteria that matter to the business, give each a weight, score every idea against each criterion and rank by the weighted total. Costs can be criteria with negative weight. It makes the trade-offs explicit and lets stakeholders argue about weights instead of features.

## How to use it

- Agree the criteria and weights before scoring any idea.
- Keep criteria few (four to six) and distinct.

## Watch out for

- Criteria that overlap and double-count.
- Tweaking weights until a favorite wins.

## What the framework articles say

These are vendor and practitioner articles, not neutral sources.

- Also called benefit vs cost: score initiatives on benefit and cost criteria and weight the ones that matter more. (see [[productplan-37-pm-frameworks]])
- Called weighted impact scoring: pick criteria, give each a percentage weight, score and rank. (see [[neal-cabage-21-pm-frameworks]])
