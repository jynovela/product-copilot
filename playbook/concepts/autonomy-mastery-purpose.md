# Autonomy, mastery and purpose

## What it is

General knowledge: Daniel Pink's model: for creative, non-routine work, contingent rewards often backfire. People do their best work with autonomy (control over how), mastery (getting better) and purpose (meaning).

## How to use it

- Give teams problems, not tasks.
- Make growth visible.
- Connect work to customer impact.

## Watch out for

- Bonuses tied to output for creative work.
