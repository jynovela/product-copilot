# Distanced self-talk

## What it is

General knowledge: Stepping back from your own situation, by using your name or "you", imagining advising a friend, or picturing the future, reduces rumination and improves judgment.

## How to use it

- Before a hard moment, coach yourself in the second person.
- Ask how you'll see this in a year.

## Watch out for

- Venting that just relives the problem.
