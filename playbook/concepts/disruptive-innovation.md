# Disruptive innovation

## What it is

General knowledge: Incumbents improve products for their best customers (sustaining innovation) until they overshoot what most customers need. Entrants start with simpler, cheaper, "worse" products for low-end or new customers, then improve until they take the mainstream.

## How to use it

- Watch low-end and non-consumption markets, not just direct rivals.
- Ask where you overshoot mainstream needs.
- Consider a separate team or unit for a disruptive bet.

## Watch out for

- Calling every new competitor "disruptive".
