# Value Stick

## What it is

From Felix Oberholzer-Gee, as described by HBS Online (which sells his course). Four points on a vertical stick, top to bottom: willingness to pay (WTP), price, cost, and willingness to sell (WTS, the lowest price suppliers accept). WTP − price is customer delight; price − cost is firm margin; cost − WTS is supplier surplus. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-pricing]])

## How to use it

- Four ways to raise margin: raise price, raise customers' WTP, pay suppliers less, or lower suppliers' WTS. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-pricing]])
- Prefer raising WTP and lowering WTS: they make the stick longer and create value for everyone, while the other two only move value around. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-pricing]])
- Use it with the floor-and-ceiling view: cost is the floor, WTP the ceiling, competitors' prices sit in between (BDC, Product Management Journal, Qualtrics). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-pricing]])

## Watch out for

- It explains where value sits; it doesn't tell you the number. Pair it with willingness-to-pay research. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-pricing]])
