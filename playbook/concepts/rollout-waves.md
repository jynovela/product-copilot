# Rollout waves

## What it is

Product School's launch sequence: internal alpha (your own team checks basic value); private beta (a stable version for invited customers); limited release for one segment (prove the GTM model before scaling); general availability (live for all target customers); follow-up campaigns (deeper adoption, upsell, new segments). "Each wave has its own goals, its own feedback loops, and its own decision points about whether to expand, adjust, or pause." (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-go-to-market]])

## How to use it

- Decide what to look at in week one, month one and quarter one (Product School). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-go-to-market]])
- Activation matters more than signups at launch; judge outcomes, not an on-time launch (Product School, Pragmatic). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-go-to-market]])
- In this wiki, a feature counts as launched on the first day the target segment can use it without an invitation; invite-only betas are rollout steps before it. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-go-to-market]])

## Watch out for

- No source in the batch defines launch tiers or separates deploy, release and launch. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-go-to-market]])

## What Escaping the Build Trap adds

Source note: from secondary summaries of the book, not the book text. Chapter numbers are the book's.

- Perri's roadmap stages (Build Trap ch20):
  - **Experiment:** problem and solution exploration; no production code.
  - **Alpha:** production code live for a small set of users who know it
    may change or be killed. Tests whether customers want it.
  - **Beta:** wider, but still a subset. Tests technical scalability.
    Optional. Rarely killed unless it is unstable.
  - **Generally available:** open to all customers.
- Splitting users into alpha and beta groups is also how Perri gives teams
  room to fail safely (ch22).

## Contradictions

- Product School's internal alpha is your own team (see
  [[2026-11-24-research-digest-go-to-market]]). Perri's alpha is live for a
  small set of real users (Build Trap ch20).
