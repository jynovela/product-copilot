# Bundling and loss leaders

## What it is

Bundling sells several items at one price (Happy Meal, Microsoft Office, Disney+ bundle). A loss leader sells one item at little or no margin to draw buyers to profitable ones (razors and printers). Predatory pricing, pricing low to destroy rivals, is the illegal extreme, often judged in the US with the Areeda-Turner test (Qualtrics). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-pricing]])

## How to use it

- Bundles raise perceived value and convenience and can move slow stock alongside a best-seller (Qualtrics). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-pricing]])
- Related structures: promotional pricing for short-term cash or acquisition (LogRocket); buy-one-get-one (Nationwide); volume pricing such as 5% off at 500 units and 10% at 1,000 (Shopify); segment pricing for students or over-60s (Product Management Journal). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-pricing]])

## Watch out for

- Volume pricing risks overstock (Qualtrics). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-pricing]])
- Mature telecoms use price complexity to make comparison hard (Product Management Journal); Inference: that erodes trust. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-pricing]])
