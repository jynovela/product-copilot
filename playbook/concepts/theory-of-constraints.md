# Theory of Constraints

## What it is

General knowledge: From Goldratt's The Goal: every system has a constraint. Identify it, exploit it, subordinate everything else to it, elevate it, then repeat. Improving non-bottlenecks doesn't raise throughput.

## How to use it

- Find where work waits longest in your delivery process.
- Fix that step before optimizing others.

## Watch out for

- Optimizing local efficiency everywhere.
