# Vroom-Yetton-Jago model

## What it is

A decision tree for decisions made for a team. Seven yes/no questions lead to one of five styles: Autocratic A1 (decide alone on what you know), A2 (get specific information, then decide), Consultative C1 (ask people one by one, then decide), C2 (discuss as a group, then decide yourself), Collaborative G2 (facilitate the group to consensus) (Atlassian). Stewart's simpler version has directive, consultative and consensus styles. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-decisions]])

## How to use it

- Use it when unsure how much to involve the team; leaders gravitate to one or two styles and should learn when to switch (Stewart). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-decisions]])

## Watch out for

- Critics call it rigid (Atlassian). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-decisions]])
