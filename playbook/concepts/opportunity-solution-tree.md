# Opportunity solution tree

## What it is

General knowledge: A tree with one desired outcome at the root, customer opportunities (needs, pains, desires) beneath it, candidate solutions under each opportunity, and assumption tests under each solution.

## How to use it

- Pick one measurable outcome the team owns.
- Fill opportunities from customer interview stories, not brainstorms.
- Choose one target opportunity, compare at least three solutions, and test their riskiest assumptions.

## Watch out for

- Opportunities phrased as solutions in disguise.
- Jumping to one solution without comparing alternatives.
