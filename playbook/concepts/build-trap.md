# Build trap

## What it is

General knowledge: When a company focuses on output (shipping features) rather than outcomes (customer and business results), it ships more and more without knowing whether any of it helped.

## How to use it

- Tie each roadmap item to an outcome and a metric.
- Review outcomes after launch, not just launch dates.

## Watch out for

- Velocity as the main success metric.
