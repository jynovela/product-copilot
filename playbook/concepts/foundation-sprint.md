# Foundation sprint

## What it is

General knowledge: A short, structured workshop where a founding team agrees on the basics (customer, problem, competitors, advantage), picks how it will differentiate, compares approaches, and writes a founding hypothesis to test, for example with a design sprint.

## How to use it

- Use at the start of a new product or bet.
- End with a written hypothesis you can test.

## Watch out for

- Treating the hypothesis as a fact once written.
