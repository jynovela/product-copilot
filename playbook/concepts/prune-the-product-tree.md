# Prune the product tree

## What it is

General knowledge: One of Luke Hohmann's Innovation Games. Draw a tree: the trunk is the current product and the branches are product areas. Participants add features as leaves. Where leaves cluster shows where people want growth, and a lopsided tree shows imbalance.

## How to use it

- Use it early in planning with customers or sales teams.
- Group the branches clearly before you start, or the session runs long.

## Watch out for

- It produces no ranking; you still have to decide.
- Unclear branches make the result hard to read.

## What the framework articles say

These are vendor and practitioner articles, not neutral sources.

- Developed by "Bruce Hollman"; trunk is existing features, outermost branches are the next release, inner branches are not yet on the roadmap; no quantitative output. (see [[tempo-9-prioritization-frameworks]])

## Contradictions

- Tempo credits the game to "Bruce Hollman" (see [[tempo-9-prioritization-frameworks]]). General knowledge: it's Luke Hohmann's, from Innovation Games, the same source as Buy a Feature. Tempo also puts the next release on the outermost branches; my recollection is that leaves nearer the trunk are nearer-term. Confidence: lower on the branch layout.
