# OODA loop

## What it is

Observe, orient, decide, act, then repeat. From combat pilots; Naik suggests it for outages, PR crises or backlash after a launch. Simon Cast compares his experimentation cycle (plan, implement, monitor, act) to it (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-experiments]]).

## How to use it

- Keep loops short when the situation changes faster than a full process can run. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-decisions]])
- Experts in real time often recognize a familiar pattern and act instead of comparing all options (Gary Klein's recognition-primed decision model, via Naik). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-decisions]])

## Watch out for

- Fast loops can skip the decision framing that slower processes force; switch back to a full process once the crisis is over (Inference). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-decisions]])
