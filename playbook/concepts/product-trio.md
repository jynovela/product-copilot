# Product trio

## What it is

General knowledge: The product manager, designer and tech lead share responsibility for discovery: talking to customers, choosing opportunities and testing ideas, so that value, usability and feasibility are weighed together.

## How to use it

- Interview customers as a trio, or rotate who attends.
- Make discovery decisions together and record them.

## Watch out for

- The PM doing discovery alone and handing specs to the others.
