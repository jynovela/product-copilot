# DHM model (Gibson Biddle)

## What it is

Gibson Biddle's (ex-Netflix) strategy question: how will the product Delight customers in Hard-to-copy, Margin-enhancing ways? It appears only secondhand in the research: Dragonboat names it with a wrong expansion, and Hustle Badger lists Biddle's essay as further reading. Biddle's GEM (growth, engagement, monetization) and GLEe models are also named by Dragonboat. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-strategy]])

## How to use it

- General knowledge: test each strategic bet against all three words; a delight that is easy to copy or loses money isn't strategy.

## Watch out for

- Dragonboat expands DHM as "Differentiation, Hub, Modularity"; that is wrong. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-strategy]])
