# Statistical traps

## What it is

General knowledge: Metrics can mislead through biased samples, picking mean versus median, tiny samples, truncated chart axes, comparing unrelated figures, and mistaking correlation for causation.

## How to use it

- Ask about the sample and its size.
- Check which average and which baseline.
- Pair metrics so one can't improve at another's expense.

## Watch out for

- Reporting the metric that tells the best story.
