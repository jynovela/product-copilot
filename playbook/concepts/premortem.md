# Premortem

## What it is

General knowledge: Before committing, the team imagines it's months later and the project has failed, and each person writes down reasons. It surfaces risks people hesitate to raise.

## How to use it

- Run it at kickoff in 20 to 30 minutes.
- Turn the top reasons into risks with owners.

## Watch out for

- Running it after the decision is locked.

## What the research digests say

- Imagine the decision has failed and ask why, before committing (Naik, Abraham). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-decisions]])
- Annie Duke uses the premortem to set [[kill-criteria]]: signals tied to actions decided in advance. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-decisions]])
- Manheim applies the same move to metrics: imagine how a metric will be gamed and design against it (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-metrics]]).
