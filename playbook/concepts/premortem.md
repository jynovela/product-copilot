# Premortem

## What it is

General knowledge: Before committing, the team imagines it's months later and the project has failed, and each person writes down reasons. It surfaces risks people hesitate to raise.

## How to use it

- Run it at kickoff in 20 to 30 minutes.
- Turn the top reasons into risks with owners.

## Watch out for

- Running it after the decision is locked.
