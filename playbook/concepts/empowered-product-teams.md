# Empowered product teams

## What it is

General knowledge: Feature teams build what stakeholders ask for and are measured on output. Empowered teams are given a problem and context (vision, strategy, objectives), decide how to solve it, and are accountable for results.

## How to use it

- Write team objectives as outcomes.
- Leaders share strategy and context and coach rather than assign features.
- Let the team own discovery as well as delivery.

## Watch out for

- Calling a team empowered while still handing it a fixed roadmap.
