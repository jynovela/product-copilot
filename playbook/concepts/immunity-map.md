# Immunity to change map

## What it is

General knowledge: Four columns: (1) improvement goal, (2) what you do or don't do instead, (3) hidden competing commitments those behaviors serve, (4) big assumptions behind them. Then test the assumptions with small experiments.

## How to use it

- Fill the map for a goal you keep failing at.
- Design a small, safe test of one big assumption.

## Watch out for

- Stopping at column 2 and just trying harder.
