# Positioning

## What it is

General knowledge: Positioning is how customers place your product in their minds compared to alternatives. Being first in a category, or creating a new one, beats being slightly better.

## How to use it

- Write: for [customer] who [need], [product] is a [category] that [benefit], unlike [alternative].
- Choose the few dimensions where you'll be clearly different.
- Position relative to the leader.

## Watch out for

- Trying to be everything to everyone.
- Line extensions that blur a strong position.

## What the research digests say

- Product School: positioning is "the slot you claim in your customer's mental market map": the category, the alternatives you're compared against, and why you're the better choice in that context. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-go-to-market]])
- Positioning comes before messaging; it is strategic, messaging is how it is said (Dovetail). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-go-to-market]])
- "You do not need to be everything. You do need to be something specific for someone" (Product School). (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-go-to-market]])
- Kaizen separates known competitors from competitive alternatives: what people would use if neither you nor rivals existed. (see [[2026-11-24-research-digest-go-to-market]])
