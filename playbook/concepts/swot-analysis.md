# SWOT analysis

## What it is

General knowledge: A 2x2: internal strengths and weaknesses, external opportunities and threats. It's a quick way to structure a discussion, but it doesn't produce a strategy on its own.

## How to use it

- Use it to gather inputs, then turn them into a diagnosis and choices.
- Make every item specific and backed by evidence.

## Watch out for

- Long generic lists that lead to no decision.

## What the framework articles say

These are vendor and practitioner articles, not neutral sources.

- A planning framework for deciding whether to start an initiative, using a 2x2 grid. (see [[productplan-37-pm-frameworks]])
