# Design thinking

## What it is

General knowledge: Start from people's needs, frame the problem before solving it, generate many options, and learn through cheap prototypes tested with users. The best-known version has five stages: empathize, define, ideate, prototype, test. It aims for solutions that are desirable, feasible and viable.

## How to use it

- Use it when the problem itself is unclear.
- Prototype the riskiest part first and test with real users.
- Write a problem statement before brainstorming.

## Watch out for

- Workshops with sticky notes and no real users.
- Treating the five stages as a strict sequence instead of a loop.

## What the framework articles say

These are vendor and practitioner articles, not neutral sources.

- Five stages (empathize, define, ideate, prototype, test) aimed at the overlap of desirability, feasibility and viability. Example: Airbnb's founders stayed in listings and found photo quality was the problem. (see [[2024-09-09-product-school-8-pm-frameworks]])
- A framework for innovation from the user's point of view; IDEO is often credited with creating it. (see [[productplan-37-pm-frameworks]])
- Grouped with the Double Diamond as a structured way to empathize, frame problems, prototype and test. (see [[2026-11-20-juan-pm-frameworks-list]])
