# Double Diamond

## What it is

General knowledge: The UK Design Council's model of the design process as two diamonds. The first explores the problem widely (discover) and narrows to a clear problem statement (define). The second explores many solutions (develop) and narrows to one that works (deliver).

## How to use it

- Check which diamond you are in before choosing methods.
- Don't start the second diamond until the problem statement is agreed.

## Watch out for

- Skipping the first diamond and jumping to solutions.
- Running it as a one-way sequence; real projects loop back.

## What the framework articles say

These are vendor and practitioner articles, not neutral sources.

- Four phases (discover, define, develop, deliver) of divergent and convergent thinking, introduced by the Design Council in 2003. (see [[2024-09-09-product-school-8-pm-frameworks]])
- Developed by the British Design Council; iterative, using divergent and convergent thinking. (see [[neal-cabage-21-pm-frameworks]])
- Grouped with design thinking for framing problems and testing solutions. (see [[2026-11-20-juan-pm-frameworks-list]])

## Contradictions

- The Product School article says the Design Council introduced it in 2003 (see [[2024-09-09-product-school-8-pm-frameworks]]). General knowledge: the Design Council usually dates it to 2004. Confidence: lower.
