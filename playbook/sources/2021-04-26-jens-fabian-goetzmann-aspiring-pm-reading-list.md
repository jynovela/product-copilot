# A Reading List for Aspiring Product Managers

- **Kind:** other (curated reading list)
- **Date of source:** 2021-04-26
- **From:** Jens-Fabian Goetzmann, Head of Product at RevenueCat

## Summary

A list for aspiring PMs: The Lean Startup, Competing Against Luck (preferred over The Innovator's Dilemma as more actionable), Principles of Product Management (his top handbook for beginners), Inspired and Cracking the PM Career (better with some experience), Cracking the PM Interview or Decode and Conquer, plus three articles.
