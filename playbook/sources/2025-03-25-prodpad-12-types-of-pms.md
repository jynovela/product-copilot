# The 12 Types of Product Managers: What Product Manager Can You Become?

- **Kind:** other (article about PM types and roles)
- **Date of source:** 2025-03-25
- **From:** Domenic Edwards, ProdPad (roadmapping software)

## Summary

Types: technical, growth, AI, group, IC, digital, data, freelance, startup, enterprise, agile and generalist. Says digital PM is now redundant because most PM work is digital, and that a data PM's job is to improve the data itself.
