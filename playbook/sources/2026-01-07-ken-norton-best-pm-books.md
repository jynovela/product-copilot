# Best Books for Product Managers [2026], by Ken Norton

- **Kind:** other (curated reading list)
- **Date of source:** 2026-01-07
- **From:** Ken Norton, executive coach for product leaders and former Google product leader, on his site Bring the Donuts

## Summary

A list of 44 books Ken Norton considers essential for product managers, each
with a short note on why. It covers product practice, strategy, design,
decision-making, communication, negotiation, leadership and personal growth.
Nine books are marked as top picks. The raw file keeps only the titles,
authors and top-pick marks; the notes are paraphrased on each book page.

## Key points

- Top picks: [[continuous-discovery-habits]], [[empowered]],
  [[high-output-management]], [[inspired]], [[product-leadership]],
  [[product-management-in-practice]],
  [[the-15-commitments-of-conscious-leadership]],
  [[the-innovators-dilemma]] and [[the-mythical-man-month]].
- Inspired is his pick if you read only one PM book; The 15 Commitments of
  Conscious Leadership is his pick for the human side of the job.
- Many picks are about people skills (negotiation, feedback, listening,
  self-awareness), not product process.
