## Summary

A 44-minute summary that follows the book's structure: the build trap, the
PM role, strategy, the product management process and the product-led
organization. Much of it reads as close paraphrase or copied passages from
the book, mixed with the author's own takeaways. Its own chapter numbers
don't match the book's (it adds a chapter on the PM role and shifts the
first part by one), so pages use the book's numbers instead.

## Key points

- The build trap is measuring success by output instead of value; products deliver value repeatedly, unlike projects and services. See [[build-trap]]
- Bad PM archetypes: mini-CEO, waiter and former project manager. See [[pm-archetypes]]
- PM work is tactical, strategic and operational; levels from APM to CPO mix them differently. See [[pm-career-ladder]]
- Strategy is a decision framework; three gaps (knowledge, alignment, effects); four levels from vision to options; quarterly and monthly reviews. See [[strategy-deployment]]
- The Product Kata's six questions, problem and solution exploration, the "ask", and done meaning the goal is met. See [[product-kata]]
- Product operations runs data, reporting, cadences and cross-team process without dictating how teams work. See [[product-operations]]
- Roadmap stages: experiment, alpha, beta, generally available. See [[rollout-waves]]
- Bias: a reader's summary. It can't show what the book left out, it may misquote, and some wording is the summarizer's.
