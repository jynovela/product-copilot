## Summary

About 39 articles on success metrics condensed into one file: 32 frameworks and lists (feature success metrics, metric decomposition, less-flawed metrics, SMART, KPIs vs OKRs, North Star, AARRR, HEART, goal trees, funnel formulas, metrics by product type and by team), definitions where the sources disagree, 14 agreed practices, 14 conflicts, and a flagged list of benchmarks. The strongest source is a peer-reviewed paper on metric failure; most others are vendors.

Every claim in the raw file names the article it came from, and vendor claims, unsourced numbers and errors are flagged. Treat it as a map of what public sources say, not as fact, and never as evidence about this product's customers.

## Key points

- Measure value, not just usage; set success and failure thresholds before launch (section 1.1).
- Decompose the goal into a formula of levers, then track volume and conversion at each funnel stage (section 1.4).
- Any measure that becomes a target gets gamed; diversify and pre-game it (section 1.5).
- KPI has seven incompatible definitions across sources, which is why the wiki says metric and target (section 2).
- Several vendor formulas are wrong (HubSpot annual churn, Dovetail MRR growth and NPS scale); the digest flags them (sections 2, 5).
