# Review queue

Changes the copilot learned but no person has confirmed yet: from sync,
morning briefs, email, and runs without stopping. One line each, oldest
first:

`- [YYYY-MM-DD] <proposed change> | from: <[[source]] or where> | confirm: <who>`

On a yes, make the change through the record workflow and remove the
line. On a no, remove the line and say why in the log entry.

## Waiting

None.
