# Log

Append-only. Newest entry at the bottom. Each entry starts with
`## [YYYY-MM-DD] <kind> | <title>`, then one line per page touched. Kind
is `ingest`, `record`, `query`, `lint`, `search`, `sync` or a skill's
name, as its workflow or skill says. Playbook changes go in
`playbook/playbook-log.md`.
