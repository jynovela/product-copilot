# The Product Manager's Essential Reading List, by Ry Sullivan

- **Kind:** other (curated reading list)
- **Date of source:** 2025-03-06
- **From:** Ry Sullivan, a product manager writing on Medium

## Summary

A reading list meant to build product thinking rather than teach process
and tools. It groups 27 books into sections: introduction to the
discipline, user psychology and behavior, designing products, product
building and innovation, marketing, strategy, business decisions and
finance, self-improvement and thinking about thinking, and stories about
building products. It adds 6 blog posts and essays. The list gives no note
for each book.

## Key points

- The author argues that the purpose of the PM role is to deliver solutions
  people love, and that processes and tools (scrum, story points, roadmaps)
  are activities, not the purpose. Inference: this matches the outcome
  focus in [[build-trap]].
- Building something yourself is the best way to learn product thinking;
  reading comes second.
- Four books overlap with Ken Norton's list: [[inspired]],
  [[dont-make-me-think]], [[the-design-of-everyday-things]] and
  [[creativity-inc]].
- New areas the list adds to the playbook: [[user-psychology-and-behavior]]
  and [[startups-and-business-building]].
