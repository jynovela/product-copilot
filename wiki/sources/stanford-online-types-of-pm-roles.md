# Types of Product Management Roles: Finding Your Fit in a Diverse Field

- **Kind:** other (article about PM types and roles)
- **Date of source:** not shown
- **From:** Stanford Online, quoting coach Priyanka Upadhyay and author Alex Mitchell

## Summary

Upadhyay's considerations: generalist, inbound or outbound, B2C or B2B, technical depth, domain or industry focus, growth PM and adoption PM. Mitchell's archetypes: technical, analytic, marketing, get-stuff-done and visionary. Stresses that the categories overlap and that there is no industry standard.

## Key points

- 13 claims about PM types extracted.
- Bias: University course marketing; promotes Stanford Online programs. Opening salary and hiring figures were left out.
