# 18 Types of Product Managers: Entry-Level to Executive

- **Kind:** other (article about PM types and roles)
- **Date of source:** 2024-04-03
- **From:** Carlos Gonzalez de Villaumbrosia, Product School

## Summary

Covers entry roles, specializations (technical, digital, growth, data, product marketing, UX/product design manager, AI) and leadership levels (senior, staff, product lead, group, director, principal, VP, SVP, CPO), with responsibilities and challenges for each. Lists product ops, product specialist, product analyst and product owner as product-adjacent.

## Key points

- 12 claims about PM types extracted.
- Bias: Training-company content. Promotes its own courses and certifications. Salary figures were left out.
