## Summary

About 31 articles on product strategy condensed into one file: 24 frameworks and models (Perri's canvas, Pichler's Product Vision Board, framework, Strategy Stack and life cycle, Murphy's four steps, Hustle Badger's seven steps, Helmer's 7 Powers, where to play and how to win, saying no, strategy-type lists, triggers to rethink), definitions, 10 agreed practices, 12 conflicts, and flagged examples and errors.

Every claim in the raw file names the article it came from, and vendor claims, unsourced numbers and errors are flagged. Treat it as a map of what public sources say, not as fact, and never as evidence about this product's customers.

## Key points

- Strategy is choices: it says what you won't do (practice 1).
- The roadmap comes after the strategy and implements it (practice 5).
- Treat a new strategy as a hypothesis and review it at least quarterly, starting from its assumptions (practices 6, 7).
- Strategy-type lists disagree and several of their entries contradict the saying-no sources (section 1.17).
- Mission and vision mean opposite things in different sources (section 2).
