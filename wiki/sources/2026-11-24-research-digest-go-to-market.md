## Summary

About 16 web pieces on go-to-market condensed into one file: definitions, GTM vs marketing and product strategy, when a GTM plan is needed, component lists, ten process sequences, motions and growth models, channels, the buying center, rollout waves, positioning and messaging, competitive analysis, pricing and enablement inside GTM, success metrics, B2B vs B2C, 10 agreed practices and 10 conflicts. Every source is a vendor, agency, research firm or course seller.

Every claim in the raw file names the article it came from, and vendor claims, unsourced numbers and errors are flagged. Treat it as a map of what public sources say, not as fact, and never as evidence about this product's customers.

## Key points

- Start GTM during development, pick a narrow target first, and differentiate against named alternatives (practices 1, 2, 4).
- Match the motion (product-led, sales-led, partner-led, hybrid) to the product, price and buying process (section 2.5).
- Roll out in waves from internal alpha to general availability, each with its own decision point (section 2.8).
- Sources disagree on who owns GTM and whether it is one plan per launch or a lasting system (conflicts 1, 2).
- No source defines launch tiers or separates deploy, release and launch (section 7).
