# Which Type of Product Manager Are You? An Exploration of Product Manager Archetypes

- **Kind:** other (article about PM types and roles)
- **Date of source:** 2024-03-05
- **From:** Adam Fishman, Fishman AF newsletter

## Summary

Builds on Casey Winters and Fareed Mosavat's four types of product work after product-market fit (feature, growth, scaling, PMF expansion). For each archetype gives skills, the work it is great at and the work it is less good at. Mentions Shreyas Doshi's leader archetypes and Ravi Mehta's PM shapes.

## Key points

- 6 claims about PM types extracted.
- Bias: Practitioner newsletter by a growth advisor. Built from the author's experience; the archetypes are not tested. Links to Reforge, where the author has written.
