# Types of Product Managers: Which Specialization Will Ignite Your Career?

- **Kind:** other (article about PM types and roles)
- **Date of source:** 2023-01-23
- **From:** Ally Mexicotte, Medium

## Summary

Written from the author's own job search. Says growth PM work only makes sense after product-market fit, enterprise PMs are rare at startups, and platform PMs scale several products on one platform. Admits she hasn't met anyone in product ops.

## Key points

- 11 claims about PM types extracted.
- Bias: Personal blog. Opinionated and based on one career; the other-titles lists for PMM and consumer PM repeat the growth list.
