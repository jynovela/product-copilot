## Summary

A short practical book on learning from customer conversations. Chapters:
1 The Mom Test, 2 Avoiding bad data, 3 Asking important questions, 4 Keeping
it casual, 5 Commitment and advancement, 6 Finding conversations, 7 Choosing
your customers, 8 Running the process. Method pages cite it as `Mom Test chN`.

## Key points

- Ask about their life and specific past events, never your idea or their future. See [[mom-test-interviews]]
- Compliments, fluff and feature requests are bad data; deflect, anchor and dig. See [[interview-bad-data]]
- A meeting succeeds only with a commitment of time, reputation or money. See [[commitment-and-advancement]]
- Slice segments until you know who to talk to and where to find them. See [[customer-slicing]]
- Prefer casual chats and warm intros; frame meetings in five parts. See [[finding-customer-conversations]]
- Prep three big questions as a team, take coded notes, review together. See [[customer-learning-process]]
