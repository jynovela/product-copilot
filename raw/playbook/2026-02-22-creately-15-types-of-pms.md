- **Technical product manager (TPM)**: Manages tech-heavy products such as AI, cloud or API products; needs strong technical acumen and knowledge of software architecture.
- **Growth product manager**: Scales products through acquisition, retention and monetization; common at SaaS companies with freemium models.
- **Platform product manager**: Oversees internal platforms, APIs and infrastructure, including integration with external partners.
- **Data product manager**: Leads analytics, machine learning and AI-driven decision-making; key in fintech and healthcare.
- **AI product manager**: Integrates AI and ML into products; needs a solid base in AI/ML concepts; listed as an emerging specialization.
- **Enterprise (B2B) vs consumer (B2C) PM**: Consumer PMs refine customer-facing products such as e-commerce, social or mobile apps; enterprise PMs manage B2B products with long sales cycles such as ERP, security or HR software.
- **Generalist vs specialist PM**: Startups need versatile PMs; mature companies benefit from specialists such as technical or growth PMs.
- **PM career ladder**: The hierarchy defines seniority and authority; types describe specialization. Senior and group PM roles involve strategy, mentoring and stakeholder work.
- **Product operations**: Optimizes product processes and workflows and acts as a communication hub.
- **Product marketing manager (PMM)**: Aligns positioning with market needs to drive adoption and revenue; listed as a PM type.
