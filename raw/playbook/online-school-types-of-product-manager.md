# Learn about the different types of product manager: extract on PM types

- Source: An online school's course chapter (probably OpenClassrooms; not named in the paste)
- Date: not shown
- Pasted into the project by the user on 2026-11-21
- This file keeps only short paraphrases of what the article says about each PM type or role. Salary and compensation content is left out. The article's own text is not copied here.

- **Technical product manager (TPM)**: A technical PM job is a PM role filled by a technical person; it matters when the product can't be grasped without deep technical detail.
- **Enterprise (B2B) vs consumer (B2C) PM**: B2B PMs usually need domain knowledge, have fewer customers and higher prices, work closely with sales, and often make public roadmap commitments.
- **Types of product work**: Early-product PMs research, prototype and validate business models; mature-product PMs polish for existing customers and ensure quality.
- **Mobile PM**: Listed as a PM type or related role.
