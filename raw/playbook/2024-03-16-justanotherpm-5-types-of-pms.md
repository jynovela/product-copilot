# 5 Common Types of Product Managers: An Overview: extract on PM types

- Source: Sid Arora, JustAnotherPM
- Date: 2024-03-16
- Pasted into the project by the user on 2026-11-21
- This file keeps only short paraphrases of what the article says about each PM type or role. Salary and compensation content is left out. The article's own text is not copied here.

- **Technical product manager (TPM)**: A technical background is a must; works hand in hand with developers (example: Tesla self-driving).
- **Growth product manager**: Focuses on acquiring and retaining users with data (example: comparing influencer and banner-ad users by lifetime value at Spotify).
- **Platform product manager**: Creates technical components for multiple products and cross-product synergy (example: infrastructure behind Gmail, Drive and Docs).
- **AI product manager**: Manages AI products from chatbots to self-driving cars, with ethics and customer focus; must be comfortable with data and ML (example: Netflix recommendations).
- **Enterprise (B2B) vs consumer (B2C) PM**: Enterprise PMs focus on scalability, security and integration with systems like CRM and ERP (example: Salesforce Marketing Cloud).
- **Generalist vs specialist PM**: Specialized PMs bring deeper expertise and make teams more efficient, as doctors specialize.
- **PM career ladder**: Its FAQ gives the levels as APM, PM, senior PM, director/VP in one answer and APM, PM, senior PM, lead PM, VP/CPO in another.
- **Three types of product management**: JustAnotherPM's FAQ says they are platform, growth and innovation.
- **Company examples**: HelloPM and JustAnotherPM give stories at Tesla, Amazon, Spotify, Netflix, Google and Salesforce.
