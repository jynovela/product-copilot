- **Technical product manager (TPM)**: Almost always from an engineering or computer science background; more capabilities-focused, less customer contact, and often acts as product owner.
- **Growth product manager**: Works on KPIs and short-term targets to raise revenue; needs sales, marketing and research skills.
- **Data product manager**: In charge of all the data used inside a company and responsible for pulling insights to inform decisions; often a former data scientist or analyst.
- **Product owner vs product manager**: Technical PMs are often the product owners on their teams.
- **Product operations**: Manages day-to-day operations of the product and business and uses product data to improve them.
- **Product marketing manager (PMM)**: Calls it marketing product manager or PMM; externally focused on messaging and revenue strategy.
- **PM archetypes**: Lists the visionary PM as a rare job type focused on long-term strategy over day-to-day work.
- **UX or design PM**: Listed as a PM type or related role.
- **Digital PM**: Listed as a PM type or related role.
- **Software PM**: Listed as a PM type or related role.
- **Analytics PM**: Listed as a PM type or related role.
