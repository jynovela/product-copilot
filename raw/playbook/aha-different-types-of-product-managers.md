- **Technical product manager (TPM)**: Collaborates with engineering on core specifications and functionality; an engineer who switches to TPM brings a deep understanding of development.
- **Growth product manager**: Delivers business outcomes across growth, retention and revenue, working with business leaders.
- **Platform product manager**: Creates and optimizes technical components shared across multiple products, working with engineering.
- **Enterprise (B2B) vs consumer (B2C) PM**: Enterprise PMs focus on products for business customers, often B2B.
- **Product owner vs product manager**: Product owner is listed among PM roles: supports the development team by prioritizing the backlog and writing user stories.
- **Product operations**: Empowers the core product team by streamlining processes, improving collaboration and documenting best practices.
- **Types of product work**: Startup PMs cover a broad range of PM work across the lifecycle.
