- **Design thinking**: Grouped with the Double Diamond as a structured way to empathize, frame problems, prototype and test.
- **Double Diamond**: Grouped with design thinking for framing problems and testing solutions.
- **RICE scoring**: For ranking features when engineering capacity is tight.
- **Value vs effort matrix**: A fast visual way to spot quick wins and deprioritize low-value, high-effort work.
- **Kano model**: Separates basic expectations, performance enhancers and delighters based on user feedback.
- **MoSCoW method**: Aligns stakeholders on scope for a release or timeframe.
- **DACI and RACI**: Clarifies decision roles and responsibilities across cross-functional teams.
- **North Star framework**: The single core metric that best captures the value the product delivers.
- **AARRR (pirate metrics)**: Tracks the user lifecycle across five stages.
- **HEART framework**: Groups user-centered metrics into five categories.
- **jobs-to-be-done**: The progress a customer is trying to make when hiring a product.
- **build-measure-learn**: Uses Build-Measure-Learn and MVPs to test risky assumptions quickly.
- **opportunity-solution-tree**: Maps desired outcomes to opportunities and potential solutions.
- **working-backwards-pr-faq**: Starts with a customer-facing press release and FAQ to clarify value before building.
- **okrs**: Connects high-level objectives to measurable outcomes over a set period.
