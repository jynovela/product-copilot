# How to find the right prioritization framework for your team (with 4 examples): framework extract

- Source: Contentsquare
- Date: 2025-07-04
- Pasted into the project by the user on 2026-11-20
- This file keeps only short paraphrases of what the article says about each framework. The article's own text is not copied here.

- **RICE scoring**: Uses impact in dollars of revenue and confidence as a relative factor (1, 1.5, or 2 if twice as sure); compare items relative to each other rather than chasing exact numbers.
- **Kano model**: Plot delight against implementation effort; three categories: basic, performance and excitement features; performance features have a 1:1 relationship between team effort and satisfaction.
- **MoSCoW method**: Centers team and stakeholder input; doesn't explicitly consider revenue or workload; set tie-breaking rules first.
- **Cost of delay**: Dividing impact per week by duration gives "cost of delay divided by duration", or CD3; example: $1,000 a week over 2 weeks gives 500.
