# 8 Product Management Frameworks & How Top Companies Use Them: framework extract

- Source: Carlos Gonzalez de Villaumbrosia, Product School
- Date: 2024-09-09
- Pasted into the project by the user on 2026-11-20
- This file keeps only short paraphrases of what the article says about each framework. The article's own text is not copied here.

- **Design thinking**: Five stages (empathize, define, ideate, prototype, test) aimed at the overlap of desirability, feasibility and viability. Example: Airbnb's founders stayed in listings and found photo quality was the problem.
- **Double Diamond**: Four phases (discover, define, develop, deliver) of divergent and convergent thinking, introduced by the Design Council in 2003.
- **Minimum viable product (MVP)**: Just enough features to satisfy early adopters and gather feedback. Examples: Dropbox's demo video, Airbnb renting the founders' own apartment, Zappos photographing shoes in local stores.
- **Product life cycle**: Four stages with strategies for each; examples: Tesla Roadster, iPhone, Coca-Cola, DVD players.
- **Agile and Scrum**: Sprints of one to four weeks, daily scrum meetings, user stories and a product backlog; examples from Spotify, Microsoft Azure, Atlassian and Netflix.
- **Stage-Gate**: Five stages: discovery, scoping, development, testing and validation, launch and commercialization; gate outcomes are go, pause, revisit or kill; examples from Apple and Ford.
- **Spotify model (squads, tribes, chapters, guilds)**: Spotify's squads complete work in sprints, allowing them to test, iterate and release quickly.
- **jobs-to-be-done**: Customers "hire" products for a job with functional, emotional and social sides; examples: Intercom, and Christensen's milkshake study.
- **build-measure-learn**: Core components are the Build-Measure-Learn cycle, the MVP and validated learning; example: Dropbox's explainer video.
- **circles-method**: Developed by Lewis Lin; seven steps; useful for interviews and real product work.
