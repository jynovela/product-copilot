# Common Product Management Roles and Job Titles: extract on PM types

- Source: Pragmatic Institute editorial team (PM training company)
- Date: not shown
- Pasted into the project by the user on 2026-11-21
- This file keeps only short paraphrases of what the article says about each PM type or role. Salary and compensation content is left out. The article's own text is not copied here.

- **Technical product manager (TPM)**: Same core duties as a PM but applies technical knowledge more, for example writing technical specifications; may have an engineering background.
- **PM career ladder**: Senior PMs handle more complex projects and stakeholders; lead PMs often oversee multiple PMs; principal PMs lead strategic initiatives and mentor; group PMs manage a group of PMs; VP and CPO are often the same job.
- **Product owner vs product manager**: Similar to PMs but in agile settings with a narrower, internal scope: backlog, user stories and sprint value, while PMs look to market data.
