# A guide to the different types of product manager roles: extract on PM types

- Source: Kimberly Hale, LogRocket blog (session-replay software)
- Date: 2024-10-01
- Pasted into the project by the user on 2026-11-21
- This file keeps only short paraphrases of what the article says about each PM type or role. Salary and compensation content is left out. The article's own text is not copied here.

- **Technical product manager (TPM)**: Oversees technically heavy work, knows backend architecture, scale and performance, and often comes from software engineering.
- **Growth product manager**: Spends much of the time on market analysis with product marketing, competitive intelligence and partners to find new opportunities and markets.
- **Data product manager**: Mostly internal-facing: understands the data model and builds pipelines so others can get insights (example: Netflix recommendations).
- **AI product manager**: Works with data science on models and predictions and needs technical and data skills (example: Google Assistant).
- **Enterprise (B2B) vs consumer (B2C) PM**: Enterprise PMs research and build features for enterprise clients with sales and customer support.
- **Generalist vs specialist PM**: Start as a generalist to learn every aspect, then consider specializing once you have launched products.
- **PM career ladder**: Ladder: associate, senior, staff or lead, group PM, director, VP, CPO.
- **Mobile PM**: Listed as a PM type or related role.
- **Innovation or product strategy manager**: Listed as a PM type or related role.
