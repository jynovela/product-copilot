# 6 product prioritization frameworks and how to pick the right one: framework extract

- Source: Atlassian
- Date: not shown
- Pasted into the project by the user on 2026-11-20
- This file keeps only short paraphrases of what the article says about each framework. The article's own text is not copied here.

- **Opportunity scoring**: Customers rate importance and satisfaction; gives the formula as importance + max(importance - satisfaction, 0), then as importance + (importance - satisfaction).
- **RICE scoring**: Confidence scale of 100%, 80% and 50%; effort is time to execute; can be time-consuming and subjective.
- **Value vs effort matrix**: Calls it "value vs. effect" and labels four quadrants: do first, do second, do last, avoid.
- **Kano model**: Three parts: basic features, performance features, delighters; highly quantitative and time-consuming.
- **MoSCoW method**: A four-step process for prioritizing requirements around return on investment; frames discussions around revenue; the meaning of "won't have" is unclear.
- **Cost of delay**: Estimate revenue per unit of time, estimate time to implement, divide the first by the second; the result is "the cost to the company for not pursuing the project".
