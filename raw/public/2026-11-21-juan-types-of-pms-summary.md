- **Technical product manager (TPM)**: Works with engineers on architecture, system requirements and technical trade-offs; common in infrastructure, developer tools and deeply technical products.
- **Growth product manager**: Focuses on acquisition, activation, retention, monetization and experiments such as A/B tests.
- **Platform product manager**: Builds shared APIs, internal tools or infrastructure that enable other teams or external developers.
- **Data product manager**: Data and AI PMs specialize in analytics, data pipelines, ML models and AI features.
- **AI product manager**: Grouped with data PMs: analytics, pipelines, ML models and AI features.
- **Enterprise (B2B) vs consumer (B2C) PM**: Consumer PMs build for individuals with focus on UX, engagement and adoption at volume; enterprise PMs build for businesses with complex workflows, organizational buyers and contracts.
- **Product marketing manager (PMM)**: Owns positioning, messaging, pricing and go-to-market to drive adoption.
