# Which Type of Product Manager Are You? (types of PM guide): extract on PM types

- Source: Agilemania (agile training company)
- Date: not shown
- Pasted into the project by the user on 2026-11-21
- This file keeps only short paraphrases of what the article says about each PM type or role. Salary and compensation content is left out. The article's own text is not copied here.

- **Technical product manager (TPM)**: Has a developer or engineering background; duties include defining technical requirements, prioritizing by feasibility and conducting code reviews.
- **Growth product manager**: Expands the user base and adoption through data, A/B tests and onboarding, working closely with marketing, sales and customer success.
- **Platform product manager**: Builds platforms that support multiple products, often APIs and core infrastructure; must be scalable, secure and easy to integrate.
- **Data product manager**: Responsible for products that collect, analyze and use large amounts of data, including data pipelines and quality.
- **Enterprise (B2B) vs consumer (B2C) PM**: Consumer PMs focus on consumer needs and UX; enterprise PMs handle complex features, long sales cycles and industry regulations.
- **Generalist vs specialist PM**: Demand for specialized PMs will only grow.
- **Product marketing manager (PMM)**: Positions the product and drives adoption through go-to-market with sales and marketing.
