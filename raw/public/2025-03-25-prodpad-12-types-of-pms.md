- This file keeps only short paraphrases of what the article says about each PM type or role. Salary and compensation content is left out. The article's own text is not copied here.

- **Technical product manager (TPM)**: A PM with extra technical know-how who handles architecture, API integrations, scalability, technical debt and security compliance; not coding, usually.
- **Growth product manager**: Laser-focused on acquisition, activation, retention, referrals and revenue with their product, through A/B tests and continuous improvement.
- **Data product manager**: The mastermind of how the organization collects and uses data; the primary mission is to improve the data itself, not a product for users.
- **AI product manager**: Makes sure AI solves real problems rather than adding a buzzword; manages variability, prompt engineering, hallucinations, bias and data security; a fast-rising type.
- **Enterprise (B2B) vs consumer (B2C) PM**: An enterprise PM works inside a large organization: slower pace, red tape, many stakeholders, and integration with existing systems.
- **Generalist vs specialist PM**: Every PM does what the specialist types do; a type just singles out the focus that matters most to the hiring company. To be good, be all types at once: diversify, don't specialize.
- **PM career ladder**: The group PM is a player-coach on the people-management track; the IC PM stays hands-on and makes impact without managing people.
- **Product owner vs product manager**: Product owner is an adjacent role, not a type of PM.
- **Product operations**: An adjacent role, not a type of PM.
- **Product marketing manager (PMM)**: An adjacent role, not a type of PM.
- **Types of product work**: Startup PMs help build the company, may build a 0-to-1 product, talk to investors and work with very limited resources.
