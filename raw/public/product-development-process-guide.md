# The product development process: six stages: framework extract

- Source: Unattributed in the paste; it links to an Asana case study, so it is probably Asana's guide
- Date: not shown

- This file keeps only short paraphrases of what the article says about each framework. The article's own text is not copied here.

- **Minimum viable product (MVP)**: The final outcome of prototyping: a product with only the features necessary to launch, so the team launches quicker.
- **SWOT analysis**: Suggested at the idea stage to judge a new product concept.
- **Agile and Scrum**: Agile suits changing requirements and is faster; Stage-Gate is more thorough but slower.
- **Stage-Gate**: Good for complex or high-cost products; structured phases with formal review checkpoints; slower than Agile.
- **build-measure-learn**: Lean means build an MVP fast, minimize waste and learn quickly; best for startups and rapid validation.
- **Product development stages (six stages)**: Listed as a framework.
