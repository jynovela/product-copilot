# Which Type of Product Manager Are You? Common Job Titles in Product Management: extract on PM types

- Source: Recruiting from Scratch (a tech recruiting agency)
- Date: 2026-06-25
- Pasted into the project by the user on 2026-11-21
- This file keeps only short paraphrases of what the article says about each PM type or role. Salary and compensation content is left out. The article's own text is not copied here.

- **Technical product manager (TPM)**: Bridges technical teams and business stakeholders, owns vision, roadmap and backlog, and tracks KPIs; common in the seed to Series C engineering and AI/ML startups the agency works with.
- **Growth product manager**: Its one-line summary says growth PMs focus on promoting and selling the product; its longer section says they drive acquisition, retention and engagement through data and experiments.
- **Data product manager**: Works on products based on data, such as analytics or ML products, across their whole lifecycle, including data governance.
- **Product operations**: Focuses on managing the day-to-day operations of the product.
- **Product marketing manager (PMM)**: Often sits within marketing rather than product; owns positioning, messaging, go-to-market and demand generation.
- **Innovation or product strategy manager**: Listed as a PM type or related role.
