# Complete Guide: Types of Product Managers: extract on PM types

- Source: Product Faculty (PM training company)
- Date: not shown

- This file keeps only short paraphrases of what the article says about each PM type or role. Salary and compensation content is left out. The article's own text is not copied here.

- **Technical product manager (TPM)**: Has a technical background; strong on technical detail but has less time for marketing; ideally has a developer background plus an MBA.
- **Growth product manager**: Leads experiments on new features in existing products; startups and businesses seeking profit hire them.
- **Data product manager**: Adapted to data management; uses data to improve new products' core functionality but does little to influence how data is used across teams.
- **PM career ladder**: Gives years of experience per level: senior PM, product lead and group PM all at 3 to 5 years or more; director and VP both at least 5.
- **Product operations**: Manages day-to-day operations and product data to make operations more efficient; placed in a business group focused on marketing.
- **Product marketing manager (PMM)**: A marketing manager first and a product manager second.
- **UX or design PM**: Listed as a PM type or related role.
- **Digital PM**: Listed as a PM type or related role.
- **Software PM**: Listed as a PM type or related role.
- **E-commerce PM**: Listed as a PM type or related role.
- **Degree requirements**: Product Faculty says data PMs need an MA in data management and PMMs and growth PMs an MA in marketing.
