# Why you need to know about different product manager types: extract on PM types

- Source: Lilia Gorbachik (a practicing PM)
- Date: 2021-07-29
- Pasted into the project by the user on 2026-11-21
- This file keeps only short paraphrases of what the article says about each PM type or role. Salary and compensation content is left out. The article's own text is not copied here.

- **Technical product manager (TPM)**: A good start for developers, QA, DevOps or data scientists; common in payments, fraud detection, voice and hardware; can pair with an outward-facing PM.
- **Growth product manager**: Grows the user base and metrics through experiments, often on a team that includes sales, marketing and UX; the product is the funnel.
- **Platform product manager**: API PMs work with developer communities as well as marketing and sales; an API platform is often the start of a marketplace.
- **AI product manager**: A good start for data scientists, but AI is a tool; the goal is still the customer's problem. A data scientist she knew went back to data science because PMs spend little time on AI itself.
- **Enterprise (B2B) vs consumer (B2C) PM**: An enterprise PM manages part of a big product with less independence and must coordinate with other PMs to avoid overlapping features.
- **Types of product work**: PM types also follow company maturity: startup PMs are a Swiss Army knife, often with no PM mentor; enterprise PMs have less independence and a slower pace.
