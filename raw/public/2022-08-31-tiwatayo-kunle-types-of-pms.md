- **Technical product manager (TPM)**: Usually has an engineering background and spends more time with development and operations, less with sales and marketing.
- **Growth product manager**: Solves growth issues such as demand or supply with data, under clear KPIs and high expectations.
- **Data product manager**: Works with the data team to close gaps in users' data experience and helps everyone understand and access the data.
- **Generalist vs specialist PM**: Types are not mutually exclusive; one person can fit several.
- **PM career ladder**: Types are not seniority levels; levels include junior PM, senior PM, product lead, group PM and VP Product.
- **Product marketing manager (PMM)**: The consumer whisperer who understands users and marketing and helps set price and positioning.
- **Types of product work**: The startup PM is a jack of all trades who works with the founders on strategy and roadmap.
