# Which Type of Product Manager Are You? An Exploration of Product Manager Archetypes: extract on PM types

- Source: Adam Fishman, Fishman AF newsletter
- Date: 2024-03-05
- Pasted into the project by the user on 2026-11-21
- This file keeps only short paraphrases of what the article says about each PM type or role. Salary and compensation content is left out. The article's own text is not copied here.

- **Growth product manager**: The growth archetype has marketing and channel skills, loves experiments, and favors speed over craft; less good at zero-to-one work.
- **Platform product manager**: The internal archetype is technically inclined, measured on cost savings, and good at build vs buy and internal tooling.
- **PM archetypes**: Seven archetypes, each with key skills, the work it is great at and the work it is less good at. Growth: experiments and speed. UX-inclined: user empathy and research. Internal: efficiency, build vs buy, cost savings. General manager: leadership, P&L, go-to-market. Optimizer: incremental quantitative improvement and messaging. Technician: scale and systems, often a former engineer or data scientist. White-spacer: vision, ambiguity, zero-to-one.
- **PM archetypes**: Maps the general manager to Doshi's operator and the white-spacer to Doshi's visionary; mentions Ravi Mehta's four PM shapes; says managers should load-balance archetypes across their team.
- **Types of product work**: Summarizes Winters and Mosavat's four types of work after fit (feature, growth, scaling, PMF expansion); before fit, nothing else matters. Different people suit each type.
- **Internal or inbound PM**: Listed as a PM type or related role.
