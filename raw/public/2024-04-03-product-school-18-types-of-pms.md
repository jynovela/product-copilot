# 18 Types of Product Managers: Entry-Level to Executive: extract on PM types

- Source: Carlos Gonzalez de Villaumbrosia, Product School
- Date: 2024-04-03
- Pasted into the project by the user on 2026-11-21
- This file keeps only short paraphrases of what the article says about each PM type or role. Salary and compensation content is left out. The article's own text is not copied here.

- **Technical product manager (TPM)**: Has a strong technical background and works on APIs, data platforms or complex backends; most duties are the same as a non-technical PM, with less time for things like marketing.
- **Growth product manager**: Owns a metric rather than a whole product and runs a series of experiments, working at a micro rather than macro level.
- **Data product manager**: Manages data-driven products such as analytics platforms, AI models or BI tools; ensures data quality and accessibility and handles governance across jurisdictions.
- **AI product manager**: Identifies AI/ML opportunities, defines requirements, works with data scientists and ensures ethical use; more PMs will need AI skills.
- **PM career ladder**: Lists APM, PM, senior PM (manages a group of products and may oversee PMs), staff (often IC), product lead (new products, on time and budget), group PM (above lead PM), director (oversees senior PMs), principal (IC, after director), head or VP, SVP and CPO.
- **Product owner vs product manager**: Product owner is a product-adjacent role outside the PM hierarchy that manages the backlog with engineering.
- **Product operations**: A product-adjacent role that streamlines processes, data and tools so product teams can focus on building.
- **Product marketing manager (PMM)**: Bridges product and marketing; positions, launches and drives demand; is the voice of the customer and runs research; less involved in how the product is built.
- **Digital PM**: Listed as a PM type or related role.
- **Product analyst**: Listed as a PM type or related role.
- **Product specialist**: Listed as a PM type or related role.
- **UX/product design manager as a PM type**: Product School's entry describes someone who leads design teams and sets UX standards.
