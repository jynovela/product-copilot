- This file keeps only short paraphrases of what the article says about each PM type or role. Salary and compensation content is left out. The article's own text is not copied here.

- **Technical product manager (TPM)**: Brings an engineering background and makes sure products are built for scale, performance and feasibility (example: Tesla Autopilot).
- **Growth product manager**: Drives acquisition, engagement and retention through referral programs, viral loops and content (example: Clash of Clans referrals).
- **Data product manager**: Describes a data-driven PM who relies on analytics to optimize features (example: Amazon checkout conversion).
- **AI product manager**: Builds products with data scientists that automate, personalize and derive insights (example: Google Health readmission prediction).
- **Generalist vs specialist PM**: The generalist wears many hats across the lifecycle (example: launching Airbnb Experiences).
- **PM career ladder**: A product analyst tracks usage data; an APM assists PMs; a PM owns a product or major feature and leads by influence.
- **UX or design PM**: Listed as a PM type or related role.
